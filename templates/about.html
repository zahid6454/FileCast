What this is

This service converts files between formats — images, PDFs, documents, and data files. It's free to use, with no sign-up, no paywalls, and no usage limits.

Most conversion tools online either charge money, require an account, or quietly upload your files to a server. This service was built as an alternative: a tool that does what it says, respects your files, and doesn't ask for anything in return.

How It Works

Client-Side
The majority of tools run entirely in your browser. Your file never leaves your device — it's converted on your own computer, not on a server. These tools work offline once the page has loaded.
Server-Side
A few conversions — like Word to PDF — need software that can't run in a browser. Your file is sent over an encrypted connection, converted, and sent back. The file is deleted from the server immediately. Nothing is stored, logged, or retained.

Why it's free

This service is supported by advertising. Ads are displayed on the page but are completely separate from the conversion tools — ad scripts cannot access your files. If you use an ad blocker, everything still works normally.

There is no premium tier, no feature gating, and no plan to introduce one. The goal is to keep this service free and useful for as long as possible.
