Your Files Stay Yours

Everything we do is built around one idea: your files belong to you, not us.

We never see your files

Most tools on FileCast work right inside your browser. Your file never leaves your device — it's converted on your computer, not on our servers.

Nothing is uploaded. Nothing is sent over the internet. When you close the tab, it's like the conversion never happened.

Look for the Your Files Stay Yours badge — it means the tool works entirely on your device.

Some tools need a quick handoff

A few conversions (like Word to PDF) need more power than a browser can provide. For these tools, your file takes a short round trip:

  • Your file is sent securely to our server
  • We convert it and send it back to you
  • Your file is deleted instantly — we don't keep a copy

We never look at, save, or share what you send. The entire process takes seconds, and nothing is left behind.

Look for the Files encrypted, processed, deleted immediately badge — it means the tool uses a secure server round trip.

What we promise

  • We never store your files
  • We never look at what you convert
  • We never ask you to create an account
  • We never sell your information
  • We never block features behind a paywall

How we keep FileCast free

FileCast is free because we show (or will show) ads. We also use basic analytics to understand which tools people find useful, so we can make them better. None of this is connected to your files in any way.

If you use an ad blocker, everything still works exactly the same. We'll never restrict features because of it.

Questions?

If anything about how we handle your files is unclear, reach out. We'd rather explain too much than leave you guessing.
