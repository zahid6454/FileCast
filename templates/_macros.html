A half-configured overlay (enabled, publisher set, slot id blank) would otherwise emit ``, which fails opaquely at Google's end. It can therefore render fewer units than the CSP allows for — one slot configured, the other not — but never more, and never any when the CSP has not opened. The direction matters: the reverse (a unit whose origins are blocked) is a broken ad, and a CSP opened for zero units is §1.1's fail-silently bug. Both ids are attribute-injected, which is why site_settings.py validates publisher_id as `^ca-pub-\d{16}$` and slot ids as `^\d+$` — neither can carry a quote or an angle bracket, and Jinja autoescapes regardless.
