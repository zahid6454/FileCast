## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my images here?

Yes. This tool processes your images entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server — the conversion happens on your own device. No one else can see or access your images at any point.

### Will transparency be preserved?

Yes. WebP supports transparent backgrounds just like PNG. If your PNG has transparent areas, they will carry over to the WebP file exactly as they are.

### How much smaller will the file be?

Most PNG files become 25–35% smaller after converting to WebP. Images with large areas of similar color tend to shrink even more, while highly detailed images may see slightly less reduction. The visual quality remains virtually the same.

### Do all browsers support WebP?

All major modern browsers support WebP, including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, and Opera on both desktop and mobile. The only exceptions are very old browser versions that most people no longer use.

### Can I convert multiple files at once?

Currently, this tool converts one file at a time. Drop a file, convert it, download, then click "Convert Another" for the next one. Batch conversion is planned for a future update.
