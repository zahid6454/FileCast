## What Is PNG?

PNG (Portable Network Graphics) is a popular image format known for its high quality and support for transparent backgrounds. It keeps every detail of your image intact, making it a favorite for logos, screenshots, graphics, and any image where sharpness matters.

The downside is file size. Because PNG preserves so much detail, the files tend to be larger than necessary — especially for photographs or images that don't need pixel-perfect accuracy.

### Why Convert to WebP?

WebP is a modern image format that produces significantly smaller files than PNG while maintaining similar visual quality. It also supports transparency, just like PNG. Converting to WebP can reduce your file size by 25–35% or more, which means faster-loading web pages and less storage space used.

All major browsers — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, and Opera — support WebP on both desktop and mobile.
