## What Is GIF?

GIF is an image format you've probably seen all over the internet. It's best known for short, looping animations — those funny clips and reaction images people share in messages and on social media. But not every GIF is animated. Plenty of GIFs are simple, static images used for logos, icons, and basic graphics.

GIF works well for images with flat colors and sharp edges, like cartoons or simple illustrations. It also supports fully transparent pixels, which makes it a common choice for icons and small web graphics.

### Why Convert to PNG?

PNG is the modern, universally supported format for graphics that need transparency. Unlike GIF's all-or-nothing transparent pixels, PNG supports smooth, partially transparent edges, and it isn't limited to 256 colors — so a static GIF converted to PNG can look noticeably cleaner, especially around curved or anti-aliased edges.

For animated GIFs, this tool extracts the very first frame and saves it as a PNG. This is handy when you need a still preview or thumbnail from an animation, with the transparent background kept intact.
