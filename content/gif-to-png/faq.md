## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my files here?

Yes. The conversion happens entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to a server or shared with anyone. Everything stays on your device from start to finish.

### What happens to animated GIFs?

When you convert an animated GIF, the tool captures the first frame of the animation and saves it as a PNG. The rest of the frames are not included. If you need a specific frame from the middle of an animation, pause the GIF at that point and take a screenshot instead.

### Will transparency be preserved?

Yes. If your GIF has transparent pixels, the PNG output will keep them fully transparent. PNG's transparency support is actually smoother than GIF's, since it isn't limited to fully-on or fully-off pixels.

### Will the quality change?

PNG is a lossless format, so the visible pixels from your GIF's first frame will look exactly the same in the PNG — no added compression artifacts. Since GIF is limited to 256 colors, the PNG won't gain any detail that wasn't already there, but nothing will be lost either.

### Can I convert multiple files?

Currently, this tool converts one file at a time. Drop a file, convert it, download, then click "Convert Another" for the next one. Batch conversion is planned for a future update.
