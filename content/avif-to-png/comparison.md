      <span class="panel__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-photo"></use></svg></span>
      <h3>Keep AVIF When</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Serving photos on your own website to modern browsers</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>File size and load time matter more than maximum compatibility</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need HDR or wide-color-gamut image data preserved</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="panel panel--accent">
    <div class="panel__head">
      <span class="panel__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-arrow-convert"></use></svg></span>
      <h3>Convert to PNG When</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Uploading to design tools, icon generators, or editors that don't accept AVIF</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need guaranteed-lossless output for further editing</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Transparency needs to survive in software with no AVIF support</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Sharing with someone whose device may not open AVIF</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
