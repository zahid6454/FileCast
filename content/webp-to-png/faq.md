## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my images here?

Yes. Your images never leave your device. The entire conversion happens right inside your browser, so nothing is uploaded to any server. Your files remain completely private.

### Will transparency be preserved?

Yes. If your WebP image has a transparent background or semi-transparent areas, the PNG output will keep all of that transparency exactly as it was in the original file.

### Does the quality change during conversion?

The conversion itself does not reduce quality. PNG is a format that preserves full image detail, so the PNG you get will look identical to the WebP original. If the WebP file was already compressed with some quality loss, that loss carries over, but no additional quality is lost in the conversion.

### Why is the PNG file bigger than the WebP?

PNG files are typically larger because the format prioritizes preserving every detail in the image. WebP uses more aggressive methods to shrink file sizes, which is why it produces smaller files. The larger PNG size is the trade-off for full quality and universal compatibility.

### Can I convert multiple files at once?

Currently, this tool converts one file at a time. Drop a file, convert it, download, then click "Convert Another" for the next one. Batch conversion is planned for a future update.
