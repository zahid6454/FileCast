## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to add page numbers to my PDF here?

Yes. This tool processes your file entirely in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to any server — everything happens locally on your device.

### Can I choose where the page numbers appear?

Yes. Pick from six positions — bottom or top, each aligned left, center, or right.

### Can I start numbering from something other than 1?

Yes. Set any starting number, which is useful for documents where a cover page or table of contents shouldn't count as page 1.

### What formats are available?

Three: a plain number ("1," "2," "3"), "Page 1," "Page 2," and so on, or "Page 1 of 12" style numbering that also shows the total page count.

### Will this overwrite page numbers that are already in my PDF?

It adds a new set of numbers on top of the existing page content — it doesn't detect or remove numbers that were already part of the document. If your PDF already has numbers baked in from its source, check the result to make sure the two sets don't overlap or conflict before using it.
