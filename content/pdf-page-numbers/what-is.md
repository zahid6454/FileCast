## What Is PDF Page Numbers?

This tool stamps a page number onto every page of a PDF, in the position, starting number, and format you choose. It's useful for PDFs that were exported without page numbers, or assembled from multiple sources that each had their own numbering — or none at all.

Page numbers make a document easier to navigate, reference, and keep in order once it's printed. "See page 14" only means something if page 14 is actually labeled.

### Why Add Page Numbers?

Long documents are hard to reference without them. A printed report with loose pages is easy to reorder by mistake if nothing marks their sequence. A document reviewed collaboratively, with comments like "on page 7, we should…", needs consistent numbering for that feedback to make sense to everyone reading it.

Page numbers also help confirm a document is complete — if the last page reads "42 of 42," you know nothing is missing.

### How This Tool Works

This tool runs entirely in your browser. When you drop a PDF onto the page, choose a position, starting number, and format, your device stamps the numbers onto every page locally, in your device's memory. Your file is never uploaded to any server. The numbered PDF is ready to download in seconds.
