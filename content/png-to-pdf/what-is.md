## What Is PNG to PDF Conversion?

PNG to PDF conversion takes one or more PNG images and packages them into a single PDF document. Instead of a folder of loose screenshot or graphic files, you get one organized document that's easy to share, print, and archive.

PNG is the format of choice for screenshots, diagrams, and graphics with sharp edges or transparency, so this tool is especially handy for turning a set of screen captures into a shareable report.

### Why Convert PNGs to PDF?

PNGs are great for viewing, but they don't page through like a document. Sending ten separate screenshots is messier than sending one PDF, and many places that accept uploads expect a PDF, not a batch of image files.

Combining PNGs into a PDF also locks in their order. A PDF's pages stay in the sequence you set, while a batch of individually attached images can arrive shuffled or out of order.

### How This Tool Works

This tool runs entirely in your browser. When you add your PNG images, your device assembles them into a PDF document — all locally, in your device's memory. Your files are never uploaded to any server. Arrange the images in the order you want, and the finished PDF is ready to download in seconds.
