## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my PNGs here?

Yes. This tool processes your images entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server — everything happens locally on your device. No one else can see or access your images during or after conversion.

### Does this tool only accept PNG files?

Yes — this tool is dedicated to PNG files specifically. If you have a mix of formats, including JPG or WebP, use the general [Image to PDF](/convert/image-to-pdf/) converter instead.

### What happens to transparent areas in my PNG?

Each PNG becomes a full page in the PDF, so any transparent areas will show as white in the finished document — the same way they'd look if you opened the PNG over a white background. If your image relies on transparency to look correct, consider whether a PDF page is the right destination before converting.

### Does the image quality change?

No. Your images are placed into the PDF at their original resolution and quality. The tool does not compress or resize your images — what you see in the original PNG is what you get in the PDF.

### How many images can I convert at once?

You can add up to 10 PNG images at once, with each file up to 20 MB in size. The images are processed sequentially in your browser to create a single PDF containing all of them as separate pages.
