## PNG vs PDF — When to Convert

PNGs and PDFs serve different purposes. A folder of PNG images is great for viewing and editing individually, but a PDF keeps everything together as a single document. Here is how they compare.

      <h3>Convert to PDF When</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need to send multiple screenshots or graphics as one organized document</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>The images represent pages of a scanned document or form</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You want to preserve a specific page order for the recipient</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>A website, portal, or application requires PDF uploads</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You want the images to print consistently on any device</li>
