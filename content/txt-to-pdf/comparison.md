## TXT vs PDF — When to Convert

Plain text and PDF serve different purposes. A `.txt` file is the simplest possible way to store text, but a PDF adds fixed page layout on top of it. Here is how they compare.

<table class="icon-table">
<colgroup><col><col><col></colgroup>
<thead><tr><th>Feature</th><th>TXT File</th><th>PDF Document</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td class="feature-cell"><span class="feature-cell__inner"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-sliders"></use></svg>Layout</span></td><td data-label="TXT File">Reflows to fit whatever opens it</td><td data-label="PDF Document">Fixed page size and line breaks</td></tr>
<tr><td class="feature-cell"><span class="feature-cell__inner"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-edit"></use></svg>Editing</span></td><td data-label="TXT File">Trivial in any text editor</td><td data-label="PDF Document">Requires a PDF editor</td></tr>
<tr><td class="feature-cell"><span class="feature-cell__inner"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-minimize"></use></svg>File size</span></td><td data-label="TXT File">Smallest possible</td><td data-label="PDF Document">Larger, but still small for plain prose</td></tr>
<tr><td class="feature-cell"><span class="feature-cell__inner"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-printer"></use></svg>Printing</span></td><td data-label="TXT File">Depends on the app's own settings</td><td data-label="PDF Document">Consistent margins and pagination</td></tr>

      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need the text to look the same on every device and printer</li>
