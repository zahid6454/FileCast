## What Is TXT to PDF Conversion?

TXT to PDF conversion takes a plain text file — notes, a log, a script's output, an exported chat — and lays it out as a paginated PDF document, with your text word-wrapped and split across pages automatically.

Plain text files have no page size, no font, and no page breaks of their own; they're just a stream of characters. Converting to PDF gives that text a fixed, printable page layout that looks the same everywhere it's opened.

### Why Convert TXT to PDF?

A `.txt` file displays differently depending on what opens it — a code editor, Notepad, and a phone's text viewer can all wrap lines, size fonts, and paginate differently, or not at all. A PDF fixes the layout once, so what you see is what everyone else sees, on any device.

PDF is also the format most places actually expect. Job applications, printed reports, and formal submissions rarely accept a raw `.txt` file, even when a PDF version would contain the exact same words.
