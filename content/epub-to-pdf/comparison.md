## EPUB vs PDF — When to Convert

| Feature | EPUB | PDF |
|---|---|---|
| Layout | Reflowable — adapts to screen/font size | Fixed — identical on every device |
| Best for | Reading on phones and e-readers | Printing, citing, and archiving |
| Page numbers | None (reflows instead) | Stable, citable page numbers |
| Universal viewing | Needs an ebook reader app | Any PDF viewer, everywhere |
| Printing | Not designed for it | Built for it |
| Annotation tools | Limited, reader-dependent | Wide support (Acrobat, Preview, etc.) |

### Keep EPUB When

Stay with EPUB when the whole point is comfortable reading on a phone, tablet, or e-reader — the reflowable text and adjustable font size are exactly what makes EPUB good at that job.

### Convert to PDF When

Switch to PDF when you need to print it, cite a specific page, annotate it in a PDF-only tool, or send it to someone whose device or software doesn't support EPUB at all.
