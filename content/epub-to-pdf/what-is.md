## What Is EPUB to PDF Conversion?

EPUB to PDF conversion turns a reflowable ebook file into a fixed-layout PDF with a stable, page-by-page structure — the format almost anything can open, print, or annotate, without needing a dedicated ebook reader.

EPUB is built for on-screen reading: text reflows to fit whatever screen or font size you choose, and there's no fixed idea of a "page." That's ideal for reading on a phone or e-reader, but awkward the moment you need to print a chapter, cite a specific page number, or send the file to someone whose software doesn't open EPUB at all.

### What Gets Preserved?

Chapter text, headings, and basic formatting carry over cleanly for most ebooks. Embedded images convert along with the surrounding text. Because a PDF has fixed pages and an EPUB doesn't, page breaks in the output are determined at conversion time rather than matching any specific printed edition — there's no "correct" page count to preserve, since EPUB never had one.
