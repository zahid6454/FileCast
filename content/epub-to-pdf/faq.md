## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my EPUB here?

Yes. Your file is encrypted during upload, converted on our server, and deleted immediately after the conversion completes. We never store, log, or read the content of your ebooks. No account is required and no conversion history is kept.

### Will the formatting look right?

Chapter text, headings, and embedded images convert cleanly for most ebooks. Since EPUB has no fixed pages to begin with, page breaks in the output PDF are determined by this tool at conversion time — there's no single "correct" pagination to match.

### Does this work with DRM-protected ebooks?

No. Ebooks purchased with copy-protection (DRM) — for example, from the Kindle Store or DRM-locked Adobe Digital Editions titles — are encrypted at the file level and can't be opened for conversion. This tool works with unprotected EPUB files, such as those from Project Gutenberg, your own writing, or DRM-free purchases.

### Is there a file size limit?

Yes, the maximum file size is 25MB. Most EPUB ebooks are well under this limit, even with embedded images.

### Can I convert multiple EPUBs at once?

Currently, this tool converts one file at a time. Upload your EPUB, download the PDF, then start another conversion for the next file.
