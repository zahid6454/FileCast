## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my files here?

Yes. The conversion happens entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to a server or shared with anyone. Everything stays on your device from start to finish.

### What happens to animated GIFs?

When you convert an animated GIF, the tool captures the first frame of the animation and saves it as a JPG. The rest of the frames are not included. If you need a specific frame from the middle of an animation, you may want to pause the GIF at that point and take a screenshot instead.

### What about transparency?

GIF supports transparent areas, but JPG does not. When you convert, any transparent parts of your GIF will be filled with a solid background color (usually white). If keeping transparency is important, consider converting to PNG instead.

### Will the quality change?

For photos and detailed images, the JPG version will often look just as good or even better than the GIF, since JPG supports millions of colors while GIF is limited to 256. For very simple graphics with sharp edges and flat colors, you might notice slight softening because of how JPG handles compression. In most cases the difference is minimal.

### Can I convert multiple files?

Currently, this tool converts one file at a time. Drop a file, convert it, download, then click "Convert Another" for the next one. Batch conversion is planned for a future update.
