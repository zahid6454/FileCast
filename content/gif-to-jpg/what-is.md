## What Is GIF?

GIF is an image format you've probably seen all over the internet. It's best known for short, looping animations — those funny clips and reaction images people share in messages and on social media. But not every GIF is animated. Plenty of GIFs are simple, static images used for logos, icons, and basic graphics.

GIF works well for images with flat colors and sharp edges, like cartoons or simple illustrations. However, it's limited to just 256 colors, which makes it a poor choice for photographs or detailed images. Photos saved as GIF often look washed out or grainy compared to other formats.

### Why Convert to JPG?

JPG handles millions of colors and is designed specifically for photographs and complex images. If you have a static GIF — especially one that started as a photo — converting it to JPG will usually give you a smaller file that looks noticeably better.

For animated GIFs, this tool extracts the very first frame and saves it as a JPG image. This is handy when you need a still preview or thumbnail from an animation.
