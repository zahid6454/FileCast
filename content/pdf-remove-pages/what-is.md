## What Is PDF Remove Pages?

This tool deletes the pages you specify from a PDF and keeps everything else, in its original order, as a new file. It's the opposite of extraction: instead of choosing what to keep, you choose what to leave out.

It's useful when only a small number of pages need to go — a blank scanned page, an outdated cover sheet, a section that no longer applies — and the rest of the document should stay exactly as it was.

### Why Remove Pages from a PDF?

Documents accumulate pages that stop being useful: duplicate scans, superseded cover pages, sections that were only relevant to an earlier draft. Removing them keeps the file focused and avoids sending or archiving pages nobody needs.

It's also a common step before sharing a document more broadly — removing internal-only pages before sending an otherwise-complete file externally.

### How This Tool Works

This tool runs entirely in your browser. When you drop a PDF onto the page and list which pages to remove, your device rebuilds the document without them locally, in your device's memory. Your file is never uploaded to any server. The result is ready to download in seconds.
