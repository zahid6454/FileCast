## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to remove pages from my PDF here?

Yes. This tool processes your file entirely in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to any server — everything happens locally on your device.

### How do I specify which pages to remove?

List page numbers and ranges separated by commas — for example, "2,4-6" removes page 2 and pages 4 through 6, keeping everything else.

### Will the remaining pages keep their original order?

Yes. Removing pages doesn't reorder what's left — the remaining pages stay in the same sequence they were in originally.

### Does removing pages affect my original file?

No. This tool creates a new PDF with the specified pages removed. Your original file is untouched.

### What happens if I try to remove every page?

The tool won't produce an empty PDF — it tells you that removing those pages would leave nothing behind, so you can adjust your list.
