## Removing Pages vs Extracting or Splitting

There's more than one way to change which pages a PDF contains, depending on whether it's easier to describe what to keep or what to drop.

<div class="table-scroll" markdown="1">

| Consideration | Remove Pages | PDF Extract Pages | PDF Split |
|---|---|---|---|
| You specify | Pages to delete | Pages to keep | Nothing — every page becomes its own file |
| Best when | Only a few pages need to go | A specific subset needs pulling out | You need every page separated |
| Remaining page order | Unchanged from the original | Set by the order you list | Not applicable |
| Typical use | "Delete pages 2 and 9, keep the rest" | "I only need pages 3, 7, and 10-12" | "I need every page as its own file" |

</div>
