## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my photos here?

Yes. This tool processes your files entirely in your browser. Your photos are never uploaded to a server — the conversion happens in your device's memory. When you close the tab, all data is gone.

### Does converting to WebP reduce image quality?

Slightly. WebP is a lossy format by default, so there is a small quality reduction during conversion. This tool uses a 90% quality setting, which keeps images visually indistinguishable from the original in normal viewing.

### What happens to transparency in my HEIC image?

WebP fully supports transparency, so any transparent areas in your HEIC image will carry over into the WebP file without being filled in.

### Will the file size change after conversion?

It varies, but WebP and HEIC are both highly efficient formats, so the file size difference is usually small compared to converting to JPG or PNG. The exact result depends on the image content.

### Can I convert multiple HEIC files at once?

Currently, this tool converts one file at a time. Drop a file, convert it, download, then click "Convert Another" for the next one. Batch conversion is planned for a future update.
