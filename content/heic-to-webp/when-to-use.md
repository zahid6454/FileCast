### Publishing Photos on a Website

WebP is one of the most efficient formats for web images, and most modern browsers display it natively. Converting your iPhone photos from HEIC to WebP gives you small, fast-loading images without the compatibility problems HEIC causes in a browser. If you're optimizing a whole page's load time, our [CSS/JS Minifier](/convert/css-js-minifier/) is a good next step for shrinking the rest of the page's assets.

### Keeping Transparency in a Small File

If your HEIC photo has a transparent background — a product shot or a sticker-style image — WebP keeps that transparency while staying just as compact as the original HEIC.

### Sharing on Platforms That Support Modern Formats

Many content management systems, e-commerce platforms, and design tools now accept WebP directly. Converting from HEIC avoids the "unsupported file type" errors that come from uploading straight from an iPhone.
