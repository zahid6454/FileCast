    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Publishing images on a website for faster page loads</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need small files that also work outside the Apple ecosystem</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Uploading to platforms or CMS tools that expect standard web image formats</li>
