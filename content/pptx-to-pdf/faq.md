## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my presentation here?

Yes. Your file is encrypted during upload, converted on our server, and deleted immediately after the conversion completes. We never store, log, or read the content of your presentations. No account is required and no conversion history is kept.

### Are animations and transitions included in the PDF?

No. PDF is a static format, so animations, transitions, and embedded videos are not carried over. Each slide becomes a fixed-layout page showing the final state of all elements as they appear on the slide.

### Will speaker notes be included?

Speaker notes are not included in the PDF output. The conversion captures the visual slide content only. If you need to preserve notes, consider printing from PowerPoint with the "Notes" layout before converting.

### Is there a file size limit?

Yes, the maximum file size is 25MB. Presentations with many high-resolution images or embedded media can exceed this. Try compressing images within PowerPoint before uploading if your file is too large.

### Can I convert multiple presentations at once?

Currently, this tool converts one file at a time. Upload your presentation, download the result, then start another conversion for the next file.
