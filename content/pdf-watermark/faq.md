## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to watermark my PDF here?

Yes. This tool processes your file entirely in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to any server — everything happens locally on your device.

### Can I choose my own watermark text?

Yes. Type any text you want — "CONFIDENTIAL," "DRAFT," "SAMPLE," a company name, or anything else. It isn't limited to a fixed set of options.

### Does the watermark cover up the content underneath?

No, not if you use a moderate opacity. The watermark is semi-transparent by default, so the original text and images remain fully readable underneath it. You can adjust the opacity to make it lighter or more prominent.

### Will the watermark print with the document?

Yes. The watermark is added directly to each page's content, so it appears whenever the PDF is printed or viewed, not just on screen.

### Can I remove a watermark later?

Not with this tool. A watermark added this way becomes part of the page content, the same way it would if you stamped it in any other PDF editor. To keep an unwatermarked copy, hold onto your original file before watermarking.
