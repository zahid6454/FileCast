## Common Scenarios for Watermarking a PDF

<div class="scenario-list" markdown="1">
<div class="scenario" markdown="1">
<div class="scenario__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-edit"></use></svg></div>
<div class="scenario__body" markdown="1">

### Marking Work-in-Progress Drafts

Sharing a document for feedback before it's finished invites confusion if it looks final. A "DRAFT" watermark makes the document's status obvious the moment someone opens it, no matter how far it gets forwarded.

</div>
</div>
<div class="scenario" markdown="1">
<div class="scenario__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-user"></use></svg></div>
<div class="scenario__body" markdown="1">

### Sending Client Previews Before Final Delivery

Freelancers and agencies often share a preview of finished work — a design, a report, a proposal — before the engagement is complete. A "SAMPLE" watermark keeps the preview useful for review without it being mistaken for the final, deliverable version.

</div>
</div>
<div class="scenario" markdown="1">
<div class="scenario__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-shield"></use></svg></div>
<div class="scenario__body" markdown="1">

### Labeling Internal or Confidential Material

Documents meant for a specific audience benefit from a visible reminder of that scope. A "CONFIDENTIAL" or "INTERNAL USE ONLY" watermark travels with the file even if it's forwarded somewhere it shouldn't be.

</div>
</div>
<div class="scenario" markdown="1">
<div class="scenario__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-target"></use></svg></div>
<div class="scenario__body" markdown="1">

### Branding Marketing or Promotional PDFs

Adding your company name or a promotional line as a subtle watermark across a shared PDF — a media kit, a portfolio, a lookbook — keeps your branding visible even after the file is downloaded and shared independently. Pairing it with a scannable link, like a [QR Code Generator](/convert/qr-code-generator) pointing to your site, gives recipients an easy way to find you.

</div>
</div>
<div class="scenario" markdown="1">
<div class="scenario__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-search"></use></svg></div>
<div class="scenario__body" markdown="1">

### Discouraging Unauthorized Redistribution

A visible watermark with your name, company, or a tracking reference makes it clear where a leaked or redistributed copy originated. It won't stop redistribution outright, but it removes the anonymity that makes it easy.

</div>
