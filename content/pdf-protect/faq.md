## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to password-protect my PDF here?

Yes. This tool processes your file entirely in your browser. Your PDF and the password you set are never uploaded to any server — everything happens locally on your device. No one else can see or access your document or your password.

### What happens if I forget the password?

The PDF becomes unreadable. This tool applies real password protection, not a cosmetic lock, so there's no "reset password" option built into the file. Keep the password somewhere safe, the same way you would for any other password.

### Will the protected PDF open in any PDF reader?

Yes. The password protection this tool applies is a standard, widely supported format that every major PDF reader — Adobe Acrobat, Preview, browser built-in viewers, and others — recognizes and will prompt for a password on.

### Does protecting a PDF change its content or quality?

No. Adding a password only affects whether the file can be opened, not what's inside it. All text, images, and formatting stay exactly as they were.

### Can I remove the password later if I need to?

Yes, as long as you know the password. Our [PDF Unlock](/convert/pdf-unlock) tool removes a password from a PDF you have the right to open, restoring normal access.
