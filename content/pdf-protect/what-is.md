## What Is PDF Protect?

PDF Protect adds a password requirement to an existing PDF, so it can no longer be opened without entering the password you set. Anyone who receives the file — by email, download link, or shared drive — has to know the password before they can view a single page.

This is different from restricting what someone can do with a file they can already open (like disabling printing). A password-protected PDF simply won't open at all in Acrobat, Preview, a browser's built-in viewer, or any other PDF reader until the right password is entered.

### Why Password-Protect a PDF?

Documents often carry information that shouldn't be visible to just anyone who stumbles across the file — a contract draft, an internal memo, a form with someone's contact details. A password keeps it from opening for a casual viewer: someone who forwards the wrong attachment, finds the file on a shared drive, or glances at a screen that isn't theirs to read.

It's a lightweight alternative to more involved access-control systems. No accounts, no permissions to manage — just a password shared directly with the people who need it.

### How Strong Is This Protection?

This tool applies standard, widely-compatible PDF password encryption (RC4, the classic scheme every PDF reader recognizes). It's a real, functional lock — no reader will open the file without the password — but it's not the same tier of protection as modern, dedicated encryption software. Free password-recovery tools built specifically for this PDF encryption scheme exist and can strip it in well under an hour. Treat this as keeping honest people (and casual snooping) out, not as a defense against someone determined to get in. For documents with a genuine regulatory or compliance requirement — health records, financial data subject to specific handling rules — check what your organization or the applicable regulation actually requires before relying on this alone.
