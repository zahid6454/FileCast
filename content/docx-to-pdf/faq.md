## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my document here?

Yes. Your file is encrypted during upload, converted on our server, and deleted immediately after the conversion completes. We never store, log, or read the content of your documents. No account is required and no conversion history is kept.

### Will the formatting look the same in the PDF?

In nearly all cases, yes. The conversion engine preserves fonts, images, tables, headers, footers, and page layout. Minor differences can occur with unusual custom fonts — in those cases, the engine substitutes the closest available match.

### Is there a file size limit?

Yes, the maximum file size is 25MB. Most Word documents are well under this limit. If your file exceeds it, try removing large embedded images or saving them at a lower resolution before converting.

### Can I convert password-protected DOCX files?

No. Password-protected or encrypted Word documents cannot be processed. Remove the password protection in Word first, then upload the unprotected file for conversion.

### Can I convert multiple DOCX files at once?

Currently, this tool converts one file at a time. Upload your document, download the result, then start another conversion for the next file.
