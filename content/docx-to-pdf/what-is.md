## What Is DOCX?

DOCX is the default file format for Microsoft Word documents. Introduced with Office 2007, it replaced the older DOC format and uses an XML-based structure compressed inside a ZIP container. Nearly every word processor — Google Docs, LibreOffice, Apple Pages — can open and edit DOCX files, making it the most widely used document format in the world.

### Why Convert DOCX to PDF?

Word documents are designed to be edited. That's their strength and their weakness. When you send a DOCX file to someone else, fonts might render differently, margins might shift, and the layout you carefully crafted might look completely different on their screen. PDF solves this by locking the document into a fixed visual format that looks identical everywhere — every device, every operating system, every PDF viewer.
