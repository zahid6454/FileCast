<div class="panel-grid">
  <div class="panel panel--neutral">
    <div class="panel__head">
      <span class="panel__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-edit"></use></svg></span>
      <h3>Keep DOCX When</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Use DOCX when you're still working on a document. It's the right format during drafting, editing, and collaboration.</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>If you need track changes, comments, or co-authoring with others in real time, DOCX is purpose-built for that workflow.</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Keep documents in DOCX while they're alive and evolving.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="panel panel--accent">
    <div class="panel__head">
      <span class="panel__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-page"></use></svg></span>
      <h3>Convert to PDF When</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Switch to PDF when the document is final.</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Contracts, invoices, reports, submissions, resumes — anything that needs to look the same on every screen and every printer should be PDF.</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>It's also the safer choice for sharing externally since the recipient can't accidentally (or intentionally) alter the content.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>
