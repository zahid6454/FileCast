## Common Scenarios for DOCX to PDF Conversion

<div class="scenario" markdown="1">

### Submitting Reports and Assignments

Schools and universities frequently require submissions in PDF format. Converting your Word document to PDF ensures the formatting stays intact regardless of which device the reviewer uses. Headers, footnotes, page numbers, and embedded images all render correctly.

</div>
</div>

<div class="scenario__body" markdown="1">

### Sending Contracts and Legal Documents

Legal documents need to look exactly as written — a shifted margin or missing font could change how a clause is read. PDF preserves every detail and provides a clear, uneditable record. Many digital signature platforms also require PDF as the input format.

</div>
</div>

### Sharing Resumes and Cover Letters

Your resume might look perfect in Word on your machine, but the hiring manager could see something entirely different if they open the DOCX file with different software or fonts. PDF guarantees your careful formatting reaches every recruiter exactly as you designed it.

</div>
</div>
<div class="scenario" markdown="1">
<div class="scenario__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-mail"></use></svg></div>
<div class="scenario__body" markdown="1">

### Distributing Marketing Materials

Brochures, one-pagers, and sales decks created in Word need to look professional when shared with clients. Converting to PDF locks in the layout, prevents accidental edits, and creates a smaller, easier-to-attach file for email.

</div>
</div>
<div class="scenario" markdown="1">
<div class="scenario__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-page"></use></svg></div>
<div class="scenario__body" markdown="1">

### Archiving Final Versions

When a document reaches its final version and no more edits are expected, converting to PDF creates a permanent, portable record. PDF files are the standard for long-term document archiving because they're self-contained and don't depend on specific software to open.
