## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my files here?

Yes. Your files are processed entirely in your browser on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to a server. No one else can see or access your files, and nothing is stored after you leave the page.

### What size will the JPG be?

The JPG will match the dimensions defined in your SVG file. If your SVG is set to 200 by 200, the JPG will be 200 by 200 pixels. If your SVG doesn't specify a size, the tool uses a standard default resolution.

### What happens to transparency in my SVG?

JPG does not support transparency. Any transparent areas in your SVG will be filled with a white background in the converted JPG. If you need to keep transparency, convert to PNG instead.

### Can I convert complex SVGs with gradients and text?

Yes. This tool handles detailed SVGs including those with gradients, shadows, patterns, and text. The conversion captures everything visible in the SVG and produces a JPG that matches. For best results with text, make sure any custom fonts are available on your device.

### Can I convert multiple files?

Currently, this tool converts one file at a time. Drop a file, convert it, download, then click "Convert Another" for the next one. Batch conversion is planned for a future update.
