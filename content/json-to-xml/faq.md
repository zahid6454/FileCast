## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my data here?

Yes. This tool processes your data entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server — the conversion happens locally on your device. No one else can see or access your data during or after conversion.

### What does the XML output look like?

The tool wraps your JSON data in XML tags. Object keys become element names, and arrays are wrapped in container elements with individual items inside. The output includes an XML declaration and is indented for readability.

### How are special characters handled?

Characters that have special meaning in XML — like ampersands, angle brackets, and quotes — are automatically escaped in the output. This ensures the resulting XML is well-formed and can be parsed by any XML tool without errors.

### Can I convert nested JSON structures?

Yes. The tool handles deeply nested objects and arrays without any issues. Each level of nesting becomes a corresponding level of XML elements, preserving the full structure of your original data.

### Can I convert multiple files at once?

This tool converts one input at a time. Paste your JSON, convert it, and copy or download the result. For additional data, clear the input and repeat the process.
