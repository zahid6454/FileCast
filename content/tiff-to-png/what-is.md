## What Is TIFF?

TIFF stands for Tagged Image File Format. It's a high-quality image format that captures an incredible amount of detail in every picture. Professional photographers, graphic designers, and print shops rely on TIFF because it preserves every pixel without cutting corners.

You'll most often run into TIFF files when you scan documents, receive images from a professional photographer, or work with print-ready artwork. Scanners love TIFF because it keeps every fine detail from the original page.

The tradeoff? TIFF files are big. A single photo can easily be 30 MB or more — sometimes hundreds of megabytes. That makes them slow to email, impossible to post on most websites, and tough to open on phones and tablets.

### Why Convert to PNG?

PNG is a lossless format too, so converting from TIFF to PNG doesn't throw away any visible detail. But PNG is far more widely supported — every browser, app, and operating system can open it, unlike TIFF. PNG also supports transparency, which many TIFF files carry as an alpha channel, and this tool preserves it fully.
