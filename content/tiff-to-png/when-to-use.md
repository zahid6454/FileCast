## Common Scenarios for TIFF to PNG Conversion

### Sharing Scanned Documents Without Losing Quality

Office scanners and multifunction printers often save scans as TIFF files. Converting to PNG keeps every bit of detail from the original scan while producing a file that opens in any browser or image viewer.

### Preserving Transparency from Professional Photos

Some TIFF files — product photography, design assets — carry a transparent background as an alpha channel. Converting to PNG is one of the few ways to keep that transparency fully intact, unlike converting to JPG.

### Uploading to Websites

Most websites and online platforms don't accept TIFF files. Converting to PNG gives you a widely supported format without sacrificing any image quality, which matters for print-quality assets destined for the web.

### Archiving at Full Quality

If you want to keep an archival copy of a scan or photo without TIFF's massive file size and limited software support, PNG offers the same lossless quality in a far more portable format.
