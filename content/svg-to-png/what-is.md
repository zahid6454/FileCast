## What Is SVG?

SVG stands for Scalable Vector Graphics. Unlike regular images made of tiny dots (pixels), SVG files are built from mathematical shapes — lines, curves, and colors described by code. This means an SVG image can be scaled up to the size of a billboard or shrunk down to a tiny icon, and it will always look perfectly sharp. There's no blurriness or pixelation, no matter the size.

SVGs are the go-to format for logos, icons, illustrations, and diagrams. If you've ever seen a company logo that looks crisp on both a phone screen and a large banner, there's a good chance it started as an SVG.

### What Is PNG?

PNG is one of the most widely used image formats. It's a standard picture file that works in virtually every app, website, social media platform, and document editor. PNGs support transparent backgrounds and display the same way everywhere, making them a reliable choice for sharing images.

The trade-off is that PNGs are fixed-size. They look best at the exact dimensions they were created at. Stretch them too large and they'll start to look fuzzy.
