## Common Scenarios for SVG to PNG Conversion

### Uploading Logos to Social Media or Profiles

Most social media platforms — Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X — don't accept SVG files for profile pictures, cover photos, or post images. Converting your logo or graphic to PNG lets you upload it without issues while keeping it looking clean and sharp.

### Using Images in Presentations

PowerPoint, Google Slides, and Keynote all handle PNGs reliably. While some of these tools have partial SVG support, converting to PNG first avoids display problems like missing fonts, broken layouts, or blank slides. Your images will look exactly the way you expect.

### Sharing with Others

Not everyone has software that can open SVG files. If you're sending an image to a client, coworker, or friend, PNG is the safest bet. It opens in any image viewer on any device — phones, tablets, and computers alike.

### Printing

Print shops and online print services typically want images in PNG, JPEG, or PDF format. Converting your SVG to a high-resolution PNG ensures your printed materials — business cards, flyers, posters — come out looking crisp and professional.

### Using in Documents and Emails

Word processors, email editors, and PDF tools work best with standard image formats. If you're inserting a logo into a letter, adding a diagram to a report, or placing a graphic in an email newsletter, PNG is the format that won't give you trouble.
