## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my files here?

Yes. Your files are processed entirely in your browser on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to a server. No one else can see or access your files, and nothing is stored after you leave the page.

### What size will the PNG be?

The PNG will match the dimensions defined in your SVG file. If your SVG is set to 200 by 200, the PNG will be 200 by 200 pixels. If your SVG doesn't specify a size, the tool uses a standard default resolution.

### Will transparency be preserved?

Yes. If your SVG has a transparent background, the PNG will keep that transparency. PNG is one of the few image formats that fully supports transparent areas, so your image will look right when placed over other content or backgrounds.

### Can I convert complex SVGs with gradients and text?

Yes. This tool handles detailed SVGs including those with gradients, shadows, patterns, and text. The conversion captures everything visible in the SVG and produces a PNG that matches. For best results with text, make sure any custom fonts are available on your device.

### Can I convert multiple files?

Currently, this tool converts one file at a time. Drop a file, convert it, download, then click "Convert Another" for the next one. Batch conversion is planned for a future update.
