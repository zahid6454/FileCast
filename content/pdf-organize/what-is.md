## What Is PDF Organize?

PDF Organize rearranges the pages of a PDF into a new order you specify, without adding or removing any pages. Every page from the original document stays in the output — just in the sequence you choose.

It's useful whenever a PDF's pages ended up in the wrong order — assembled from scans done out of sequence, merged from multiple sources, or simply needing a different structure than they started with.

### Why Reorder PDF Pages?

Page order matters for how a document reads. A report with its summary after its appendix, or a scanned packet with pages out of sequence from how they went through the scanner, is confusing to read even though nothing is technically missing.

Reordering fixes that without touching the content of any individual page — the fix is purely about sequence.
