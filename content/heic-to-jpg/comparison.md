## HEIC vs JPG — When to Use Each

| Feature | HEIC | JPG |
|---|---|---|
| File size | Smaller (roughly 50% of JPG) | Larger |
| Image quality | Excellent at low file sizes | Good, but degrades with compression |
| Transparency | Supported | Not supported |
| Animation | Supported (like Live Photos) | Not supported |
| Compatibility | Apple devices only | Universal — works everywhere |
| Web browsers | Limited support | Supported by all browsers |
| Social media | Usually not accepted | Accepted everywhere |
| Color depth | Up to 16-bit | 8-bit |
| Editing support | Limited (few editors support it) | Universal |

### Keep HEIC When

- Storing photos on your iPhone or iPad
- Saving space on Apple devices
- Working within the Apple ecosystem (AirDrop, iCloud, Mac)
- You need Live Photo or depth map data

### Convert to JPG When

- Sharing photos on social media (Instagram, Facebook, Twitter)
- Sending images via email to non-Apple users
- Uploading to websites or online forms
- Editing in Photoshop, GIMP, or other non-Apple editors
- Transferring photos to Windows or Android devices
- Printing photos through online services
