      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Working within the Apple ecosystem (AirDrop, iCloud, Mac)</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need Live Photo or depth map data</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

    <div class="panel__head">

      <h3>Convert to JPG When</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Sharing photos on social media (Instagram, Facebook, Twitter)</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Sending images via email to non-Apple users</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Uploading to websites or online forms</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Editing in Photoshop, GIMP, or other non-Apple editors</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Transferring photos to Windows or Android devices</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Printing photos through online services</li>
