## What Is HEIC?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the default photo format on iPhones and iPads since iOS 11. It uses the HEIF (High Efficiency Image Format) standard, which delivers the same visual quality as JPG at roughly half the file size.

Apple adopted HEIC because it saves significant storage space on devices — a real advantage when you're shooting thousands of photos. The format also supports features like Live Photos, depth maps, and transparency that JPG cannot.

### Why Convert to JPG?

Despite its technical advantages, HEIC has a major limitation: **most platforms outside the Apple ecosystem don't support it.** Windows PCs, Android devices, web browsers, social media platforms, email clients, and many photo editing tools expect JPG files.

This means every time you transfer photos from an iPhone to a non-Apple device, share images on social media, or attach photos to an email, you may need to convert HEIC to JPG first.
