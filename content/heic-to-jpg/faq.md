## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my photos here?

Yes. This tool processes your files entirely in your browser. Your photos are never uploaded to a server — the conversion happens in your device's memory. When you close the tab, all data is gone. There is nothing for us to store, see, or share.

### Does converting to JPG reduce image quality?

Slightly. JPG is a lossy format, so there is a small quality reduction during conversion. This tool uses a 92% quality setting, which produces JPG files that are visually indistinguishable from the original in normal viewing. For most uses — social media, email, printing — the difference is imperceptible.

### What happens to transparency in my HEIC image?

JPG does not support transparency. If your HEIC image has transparent areas, they will be filled with a white background in the converted JPG. If you need to preserve transparency, convert to PNG instead.

### Will the file size increase after conversion?

Usually, yes. HEIC achieves roughly 50% smaller file sizes than JPG at the same quality. When you convert to JPG, the file size will typically increase. The exact amount depends on the image content — photos with lots of detail will see a larger increase than simple images.

### Can I convert multiple HEIC files at once?

Currently, this tool converts one file at a time. Drop a file, convert it, download, then click "Convert Another" for the next one. Batch conversion is planned for a future update.
