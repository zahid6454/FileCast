## What Is Image Compression?

Image compression reduces the file size of a photo or graphic without changing its dimensions. The image stays the same width and height — it just takes up less space on your device and loads faster when shared online. This is especially useful for photographs and screenshots that are larger than they need to be.

Most images straight from a camera or screenshot tool contain more data than the human eye can perceive. Compression works by trimming that invisible excess, giving you a lighter file that still looks great. The amount of compression is up to you — a small reduction keeps quality nearly untouched, while heavier compression saves more space with a gradual trade-off in sharpness.

### Why Compress Images?

Large image files slow down websites, fill up cloud storage, and make email attachments frustrating to send. A single uncompressed photo can be 5-10 MB, while a well-compressed version of the same image might be under 1 MB with no visible difference. Whether you're managing a personal photo collection or preparing images for a website, compression is one of the simplest ways to save space and improve loading times.

### Supported Formats

This tool accepts JPG, PNG, and WebP images. You control the output quality with a slider ranging from 10% to 100%, letting you find the perfect balance between file size and visual quality for each image.

### How This Tool Works

This compressor runs entirely in your browser. When you select an image, your device processes the compression locally — your file is never uploaded to any server. Adjust the quality slider to preview different compression levels, then download the result. The original file on your device remains untouched.
