<div class="panel-grid">
  <div class="panel panel--neutral">
    <div class="panel__head">
      <span class="panel__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-photo"></use></svg></span>
      <h3>Keep BMP When</h3>
    </div>
    <p>BMP files are rarely needed in everyday life, but there are a few narrow cases where you might hold onto them:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You're working with specialized software that specifically requires BMP input</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need a completely uncompressed master copy for professional editing</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You're working on pixel art or icon design at very small sizes</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>The file is part of a legacy system that only reads BMP</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="panel panel--accent">
    <div class="panel__head">
      <span class="panel__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-arrow-convert"></use></svg></span>
      <h3>Convert to JPG When</h3>
    </div>
    <p>For nearly everything else, JPG is the better choice:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You want to share photos by email, text, or social media</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need to upload images to a website or online profile</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Your hard drive or phone storage is running low</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You received old scanned documents saved as BMP</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You want images that open easily on any phone, tablet, or computer</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You're building a presentation, document, or portfolio</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You simply want files that are faster to open and move around</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>
