## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my BMP files here?

Yes, completely safe. This tool runs entirely inside your web browser. Your images are never uploaded to any server or sent over the internet. Everything happens on your own device, so your files stay private from start to finish.

### How much will the file size shrink?

You can expect a dramatic reduction. Most BMP files shrink by 90% or more when converted to JPG. For example, a 30 MB BMP image will typically become around 2 to 3 MB as a JPG. The exact savings depend on the image content, but the difference is always significant.

### Does the quality change when I convert?

JPG uses compression to reduce file size, which means a tiny amount of detail is removed during the conversion. In practice, the difference is invisible to the naked eye for photos and everyday images. Side by side, most people cannot tell a JPG apart from the original BMP.

### Why are BMP files so large in the first place?

BMP files store every single pixel of your image without any compression at all. Think of it like writing out every word in a book without using any abbreviations. JPG, on the other hand, uses smart shortcuts to describe the same picture with far less data. That's why the same image is so much smaller as a JPG.

### Can I convert multiple BMP files at once?

Currently, this tool converts one file at a time. Drop a file, convert it, download, then click "Convert Another" for the next one. Batch conversion is planned for a future update.
