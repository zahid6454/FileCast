## What Is BMP?

BMP stands for Bitmap, and it's one of the oldest image formats around. It dates back to the early days of Microsoft Windows, when computers needed a simple way to store pictures. A BMP file saves every single dot of color in your image without cutting any corners, which means the picture looks exactly as it was captured.

The downside? BMP files are huge. Because nothing is compressed or trimmed down, even a small photo can take up several megabytes of space. A single screenshot saved as BMP might be ten times larger than the same image saved as a JPG.

### Why Convert to JPG?

JPG (also written as JPEG) is the standard image format used across the internet, phones, cameras, and just about every app you can think of. It works by smartly compressing your image, making the file dramatically smaller while keeping the picture looking great to the human eye. A JPG version of a BMP image is typically 90% smaller or more, works on every device and app, and is quick to email, text, or upload.
