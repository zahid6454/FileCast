## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my files here?

Yes. Your files never leave your device. The entire conversion happens right inside your browser — nothing gets uploaded to any server. No one else can see or access your images. Once you close the page, nothing is stored.

### What about multi-page TIFFs?

Some TIFF files contain multiple pages in a single file, which is common with scanned documents. This tool converts the first page of a multi-page TIFF. If you need other pages, you may need to split the TIFF into individual files first using a dedicated tool.

### Does quality change when I convert?

JPG uses a different approach to storing images than TIFF, so there is a small reduction in fine detail. For everyday purposes like sharing, viewing, and printing at normal sizes, the difference is virtually impossible to notice. This tool uses a high quality setting that keeps your images looking sharp.

### How much smaller will my files be?

It depends on the image, but most people see their files shrink to roughly one-tenth of the original size or even smaller. A 50 MB TIFF might become a 3-5 MB JPG. This tool uses a high quality setting that keeps your images looking sharp while still delivering dramatically smaller files.

### Can I convert multiple files at once?

Currently, this tool converts one file at a time. Drop a file, convert it, download, then click "Convert Another" for the next one. Batch conversion is planned for a future update.
