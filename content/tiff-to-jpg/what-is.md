## What Is TIFF?

TIFF stands for Tagged Image File Format. It's a high-quality image format that captures an incredible amount of detail in every picture. Professional photographers, graphic designers, and print shops rely on TIFF because it preserves every pixel without cutting corners.

You'll most often run into TIFF files when you scan documents, receive images from a professional photographer, or work with print-ready artwork. Scanners love TIFF because it keeps every fine detail from the original page. Photo studios deliver in TIFF because it gives you the sharpest, most accurate version of your pictures.

The tradeoff? TIFF files are big. A single photo can easily be 30 MB or more — sometimes hundreds of megabytes. That makes them slow to email, impossible to post on most websites, and tough to open on phones and tablets. Many everyday apps simply won't open a TIFF file at all.

### Why Convert to JPG?

JPG is the opposite. It works everywhere — phones, browsers, social media, email, you name it. JPG files are dramatically smaller, usually just a fraction of the original TIFF size. The picture still looks great for everyday use, even though some behind-the-scenes detail gets trimmed to save space.
