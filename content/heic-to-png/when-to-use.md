### Preserving Transparency for Design Work

Some HEIC photos — portrait mode shots, edited stickers — include transparency. PNG is the safest format to preserve that transparency when moving the image into a design tool.

### Archiving Photos Long-Term

If you're keeping a converted photo as a long-term archival copy, PNG's lossless compression means no visual detail is lost, unlike converting to JPG. Once you have the final archived copy, our [Hash Generator](/convert/hash-generator/) can produce a checksum so you can confirm later that the file hasn't changed.

### Editing in Photoshop, GIMP, or Other Editors

Not every editor opens HEIC directly, but virtually all of them handle PNG without issue. Converting first avoids compatibility problems before you start editing.

</div>

### Uploading to Websites and Platforms That Reject HEIC

Some websites and content platforms reject HEIC uploads outright. PNG is universally accepted and keeps full image quality intact.
