## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my photos here?

Yes. This tool processes your files entirely in your browser. Your photos are never uploaded to a server — the conversion happens in your device's memory. When you close the tab, all data is gone.

### Does converting to PNG reduce image quality?

No. PNG is a lossless format, so no visible detail is lost during conversion. The pixels in your PNG will match the original HEIC photo exactly.

### What happens to transparency in my HEIC image?

PNG fully supports transparency, so any transparent areas in your HEIC image will carry over into the PNG without being filled in.

### Will the file size increase after conversion?

Usually, yes. HEIC's compression is more aggressive than PNG's lossless compression, so PNG files are typically larger. The exact amount depends on how much detail is in the photo.

### Can I convert multiple HEIC files at once?

Currently, this tool converts one file at a time. Drop a file, convert it, download, then click "Convert Another" for the next one. Batch conversion is planned for a future update.
