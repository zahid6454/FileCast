## HEIC vs PNG — When to Use Each

| Feature | HEIC | PNG |
|---|---|---|
| File size | Smaller | Larger, lossless |
| Image quality | Excellent at low file sizes | Maximum — no detail is lost |
| Transparency | Supported | Supported |
| Animation | Supported (like Live Photos) | Not supported |
| Compatibility | Apple devices only | Universal — works everywhere |
| Web browsers | Limited support | Supported by all browsers |
| Editing support | Limited (few editors support it) | Universal |

### Keep HEIC When

- Storing photos on your iPhone or iPad
- Saving space on Apple devices
- Working within the Apple ecosystem (AirDrop, iCloud, Mac)
- You need Live Photo or depth map data

### Convert to PNG When

- You need to preserve transparency at full quality
- You're editing in Photoshop, GIMP, or other non-Apple editors
- You want a lossless archival copy with no compression artifacts
- Uploading to websites or platforms that reject HEIC
- Transferring photos to Windows or Android devices
