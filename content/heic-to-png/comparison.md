      <h3>Keep HEIC When</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Storing photos on your iPhone or iPad</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Saving space on Apple devices</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Working within the Apple ecosystem (AirDrop, iCloud, Mac)</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need Live Photo or depth map data</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="panel panel--accent">
    <div class="panel__head">
      <span class="panel__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-arrow-convert"></use></svg></span>
      <h3>Convert to PNG When</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need to preserve transparency at full quality</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You're editing in Photoshop, GIMP, or other non-Apple editors</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You want a lossless archival copy with no compression artifacts</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Uploading to websites or platforms that reject HEIC</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Transferring photos to Windows or Android devices</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
