## Common Scenarios for Cropping Images

### Making a Profile Photo

Profile photos on most platforms need a square or tightly-framed shot of a face. Cropping lets you select exactly that area from a larger photo instead of using the whole frame.

### Removing Background Distractions

A great subject can get lost in a busy background. Cropping tighter around what matters — a product, a document, a person — draws attention to it without needing any editing software.

### Fitting a Specific Aspect Ratio

Different platforms expect different shapes: wide banners, square thumbnails, tall stories. Cropping lets you select the exact area that fits, rather than letting a platform auto-crop it for you.

### Extracting a Detail From a Larger Image

Sometimes you only need one part of a photo — a section of a screenshot, a signature from a scanned page, or a single item from a group shot. Cropping isolates just that region.
