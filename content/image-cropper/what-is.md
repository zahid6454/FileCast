## What Is Image Cropping?

Cropping removes everything outside a rectangle you choose, keeping only the part of the image you actually want. Unlike resizing, which scales the whole picture up or down, cropping trims it down to a smaller area at full quality — nothing is stretched or compressed.

This tool shows a live preview with a draggable, resizable selection box directly over your image, so you can see exactly what you're keeping before you download anything.

### Why Crop an Image?

Photos often include more than you need — background clutter, other people, extra empty space, or the wrong aspect ratio for where you're posting it. Cropping lets you focus on the part that matters, remove distractions, or fit an image into a specific shape without leaving your browser.

### Supported Formats

This tool accepts JPG, PNG, and WebP images. Drag inside the selection box to move it, or drag any corner to resize it, then convert to download just that area.
