## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to crop my images here?

Yes. This tool processes your image entirely in your browser. Your file is never uploaded to any server — everything happens locally on your device, including the interactive selection preview.

### How do I adjust the crop area?

Drag inside the blue selection box to move it, or drag any of its four corner handles to resize it. The preview updates live so you can see exactly what will be kept before you convert.

### Can I use this on a touchscreen?

Yes. The selection box responds to touch the same way it responds to a mouse — tap and drag inside it to move, or drag a corner to resize.

### Does cropping reduce image quality?

No. The kept area is copied at its original resolution with no scaling, so there's no quality loss from cropping itself. JPEG output uses a high-quality setting, matching FileCast's other image tools.

### Which image formats are supported?

This tool accepts JPG, PNG, and WebP files. The output keeps the same format as your original, including transparency for PNG and WebP.
