## Common Scenarios for Image to PDF Conversion

### Submitting Scanned Documents

Many government agencies, banks, and employers require documents in PDF format. If you have scanned your ID, receipts, or forms as individual images, converting them to a single PDF makes them ready to upload without any hassle.

### Creating a Photo Portfolio

Photographers, designers, and artists often need to share their work in a clean, professional format. Combining selected images into a PDF creates a polished portfolio that recipients can scroll through as a single document — no need to download and sort individual files.

### Preparing Presentation Handouts

When you need to share slides, charts, or visual materials from a meeting, combining them into a PDF keeps everything in the right order. Recipients get one file they can print or review later, rather than a folder of loose images.

### Archiving Receipts and Records

Keeping digital records of receipts, warranties, and important documents is much easier when they are organized as PDFs. Converting a batch of receipt photos into a single PDF per month or per category creates a tidy filing system that is easy to search through later.

### Sending Images by Email

Email attachments work best with fewer files. Instead of attaching ten separate photos to an email, converting them to a single PDF gives your recipient one clean download. This also avoids the risk of images arriving out of order or getting lost among other attachments.
