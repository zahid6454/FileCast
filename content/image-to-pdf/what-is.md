## What Is Image to PDF Conversion?

Image to PDF conversion takes one or more image files — such as photos, screenshots, or scanned pages — and packages them into a single PDF document. Instead of a loose collection of image files, you get a neatly organized document that's easy to share, print, and archive.

This is particularly useful when you've captured something with a camera or screenshot tool and need to present it in a more formal, document-like format. Receipts, whiteboard notes, signed forms, and photo collections all become more practical as PDFs.

### Why Convert Images to PDF?

Images are great for viewing, but they lack the structure of a document. You can't easily page through a set of images the way you flip through a PDF, and sending ten separate image files is messier than sending one PDF. Converting to PDF gives your images the organization and portability of a document.

PDFs are also the expected format for many submissions. Insurance claims, school assignments, and business reports typically require PDF uploads, not a zip folder full of images.
