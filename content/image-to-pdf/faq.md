## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my images here?

Yes. This tool processes your images entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server — everything happens locally on your device. No one else can see or access your images during or after conversion.

### What image formats are supported?

This tool accepts JPG, PNG, and WebP images. These are the most common image formats used by phones, cameras, and scanners. Each image becomes one page in the resulting PDF document.

### Does the image quality change?

No. Your images are placed into the PDF at their original resolution and quality. The tool does not compress or resize your images — what you see in the original is what you get in the PDF.

### Can I control the page order?

Yes. Images are added to the PDF in the order you select them. If you need a specific arrangement, add your files in the order you want them to appear. The first image you add becomes the first page, and so on.

### How many images can I convert at once?

You can add up to 10 images at once, with each file up to 20 MB in size. The images are processed sequentially in your browser to create a single PDF containing all of them as separate pages.
