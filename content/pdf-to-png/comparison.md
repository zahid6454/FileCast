## PDF vs Image — When to Convert

PDFs and images serve different purposes. Choosing the right format depends on how the content will be used. Here's a comparison.

<div class="table-scroll">
<table class="icon-table">
<colgroup><col><col><col></colgroup>
<thead><tr><th>Consideration</th><th>PDF</th><th>Image (PNG)</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td class="feature-cell"><span class="feature-cell__inner"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-page"></use></svg>Multi-page support</span></td><td data-label="PDF">Yes, built in</td><td data-label="Image (PNG)">One file per page</td></tr>
<tr><td class="feature-cell"><span class="feature-cell__inner"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-search"></use></svg>Text searchability</span></td><td data-label="PDF">Text is selectable and searchable</td><td data-label="Image (PNG)">Text becomes part of the picture</td></tr>
<tr><td class="feature-cell"><span class="feature-cell__inner"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-file-plus"></use></svg>Embedding in slides or posts</span></td><td data-label="PDF">Requires extra steps</td><td data-label="Image (PNG)">Drag and drop</td></tr>
<tr><td class="feature-cell"><span class="feature-cell__inner"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Viewing requirements</span></td><td data-label="PDF">PDF reader or browser</td><td data-label="Image (PNG)">Any device, any app</td></tr>
<tr><td class="feature-cell"><span class="feature-cell__inner"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-maximize"></use></svg>File size</span></td><td data-label="PDF">Compact for text-heavy documents</td><td data-label="Image (PNG)">Larger per page for detailed content</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Recipients need to fill in form fields or add signatures</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You're archiving official documents where format integrity matters</li>

    <div class="panel__head">
      <span class="panel__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-photo"></use></svg></span>
      <h3>Convert to Image When</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need to insert a page into a presentation, slide deck, or website</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You're sharing a single page in a group chat or messaging app</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>The content will be posted on social media where PDFs aren't supported</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You want a thumbnail or preview of a document's first page</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You're including PDF content in a design project or graphic layout</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need to annotate or mark up pages using a basic image editor</li>
    </ul>
