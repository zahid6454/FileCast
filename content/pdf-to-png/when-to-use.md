## Common Scenarios for PDF to PNG Conversion

### Adding Pages to Presentations

Slide tools like PowerPoint and Google Slides handle images natively but don't always play well with embedded PDFs. Converting a PDF page to a PNG image lets you drop it directly onto a slide as a visual — perfect for including charts, certificates, or reference material in your deck.

### Sharing on Social Media

Social platforms accept images but not PDF files. If you've designed a flyer, infographic, or announcement as a PDF, converting to a PNG image makes it ready to post on Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, or any other platform without extra steps.

### Creating Website Content

Websites display images effortlessly but handling embedded PDFs is clunky for visitors. Converting key pages to images lets you show document content directly on a webpage — as a product spec, a preview of a downloadable guide, or a visual reference. If page load speed matters, pass the result through [Image Compressor](/convert/image-compress/) first.

### Sending Quick Previews in Chat

Messaging apps open images instantly in the conversation. Sending a PDF, on the other hand, forces the recipient to download and open it separately. Converting to a PNG image means a receipt, invoice, or form page shows up right in the chat where it's easiest to view.

### Archiving Visual Snapshots

Sometimes you need a frozen visual record of a document at a specific point in time. Converting to a PNG image captures exactly how the page looked — layout, fonts, colors, and all — in a format that won't change regardless of what software opens it later.
