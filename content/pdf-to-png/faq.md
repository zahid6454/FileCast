## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my PDF here?

Yes. This tool processes your file entirely in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to any server — everything happens locally on your device. No one else can see or access your document during or after the conversion.

### What image format will I get?

Each page of your PDF is converted into a PNG image. PNG preserves sharp text, clean lines, and full color detail without any quality loss, making it the best choice for document pages.

### Can I still search or copy text from the image?

No. Once a PDF page becomes an image, the text is part of the picture. You won't be able to select, search, or copy text from the resulting PNG file. If you need searchable text, keep the PDF version alongside the images.

### Will the images look as sharp as the original PDF?

Yes. The images are rendered at high quality to match the visual appearance of the original PDF pages. Text, graphics, and colors will look the same as they do in the PDF. The result is suitable for presentations, printing, and web use.

### Can I convert a multi-page PDF all at once?

Yes. When you drop a multi-page PDF onto the page, every page is converted into its own separate PNG image. You can download each page individually, giving you full control over which pages you keep and use.
