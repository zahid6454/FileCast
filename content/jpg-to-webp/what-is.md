## What Is JPG?

JPG (also written as JPEG) is the most widely used image format in the world. Nearly every photo you take, download, or receive is a JPG. It strikes a practical balance between image quality and file size, which is why it became the default for cameras, phones, and the web.

### Why Convert to WebP?

WebP is a modern format created by Google that produces noticeably smaller files than JPG while keeping the visual quality nearly identical. A photo that takes up 500 KB as a JPG might only need 300 KB or less as a WebP — same image, less storage. Every major browser supports WebP today, including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, and Opera.

### How This Tool Works

This converter runs entirely in your browser. When you drop in a JPG file, the conversion happens right on your device — your images are never uploaded to a server. Your files stay private, and the original file on your device is never modified or deleted.
