## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my images here?

Yes. This tool runs entirely in your browser. Your images are never uploaded to any server — the conversion happens on your own device. No one else can see or access your files during the process.

### Will the quality of my images be affected?

The difference between the original JPG and the converted WebP is virtually impossible to see. This tool uses a high quality setting that preserves the look of your original image while still delivering a smaller file.

### How much smaller will my files be?

Most JPG images become 25–35% smaller after converting to WebP at a comparable quality level. The exact savings depend on the content of the image — photos with large areas of similar color tend to compress even more, while very detailed images may see slightly less reduction.

### Do all browsers support WebP?

All major modern browsers support WebP, including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, and Opera on both desktop and mobile. The only exceptions are very outdated browser versions that most people no longer use. For the vast majority of visitors, WebP images will display perfectly.

### Can I convert multiple files at once?

Currently, this tool converts one file at a time. Drop a file, convert it, download, then click "Convert Another" for the next one. Batch conversion is planned for a future update.
