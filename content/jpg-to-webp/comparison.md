## JPG vs WebP — When to Use Each

Both formats handle photos well, but they shine in different situations.

<tr><td class="feature-cell"><span class="feature-cell__inner"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-target"></use></svg>Photo quality</span></td><td data-label="JPG">Excellent</td><td data-label="WebP">Excellent (comparable to JPG)</td></tr>

<div class="panel-grid">
  <div class="panel panel--neutral">
    <div class="panel__head">
      <span class="panel__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-photo"></use></svg></span>
      <h3>Keep JPG When</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need the widest possible compatibility with older devices and software</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You are sharing photos with people who may use outdated image viewers</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You are submitting images to a service or platform that specifically requires JPG</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You plan to edit the image repeatedly in photo editing software that handles JPG better</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You are printing photos through a service that only accepts JPG uploads</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="panel panel--accent">
    <div class="panel__head">
      <span class="panel__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-arrow-convert"></use></svg></span>
      <h3>Convert to WebP When</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You are adding images to a website and want pages to load faster</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need to reduce bandwidth costs for a site with lots of visitors</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You want to save storage space without losing visible quality</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You are building a web app or online project that benefits from smaller assets</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You are optimizing images for platforms that recommend or prefer WebP</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need transparency in a photo-quality image (something JPG cannot do at all)</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
