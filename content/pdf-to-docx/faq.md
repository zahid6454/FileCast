## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my PDF here?

Yes. Your file is encrypted during upload, converted on our server, and deleted immediately after the conversion completes. We never store, log, or read the content of your documents. No account is required and no conversion history is kept.

### Will the formatting look exactly the same?

Simple documents — letters, reports, forms — convert with high fidelity. Complex layouts with multiple columns, layered graphics, or decorative fonts may need minor adjustments in Word after conversion. The tool reconstructs real paragraphs and tables, not text boxes, so the result is genuinely editable.

### Can I convert scanned PDFs?

This tool works best with PDFs that contain real text (created from Word, exported from software, or digitally generated). Scanned PDFs — where pages are essentially images — will convert but the text won't be editable since there's no text layer to extract.

### Is there a file size limit?

Yes, the maximum file size is 25MB. Most PDF documents are well under this limit. If your file exceeds it, try splitting it into smaller sections using the PDF Split tool, converting each part separately.

### Can I convert multiple PDFs at once?

Currently, this tool converts one file at a time. Upload your PDF, download the Word document, then start another conversion for the next file.
