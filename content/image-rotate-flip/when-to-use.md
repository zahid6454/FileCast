## Common Scenarios for Rotating or Flipping Images

### Fixing a Sideways Phone Photo

Photos taken with a phone held on its side sometimes display sideways when viewed outside the phone's own gallery app, since not every app respects the orientation data recorded by the camera. Rotating 90° or 270° fixes this permanently, so the image displays correctly everywhere.

### Correcting a Scanned Document

Feeding a page into a flatbed or sheet-fed scanner the wrong way round is an easy mistake. A 90° or 180° rotation gets it back to a readable orientation without needing to rescan.

### Reversing a Mirrored Scan

Some scanners and camera apps (especially front-facing phone cameras) can produce a horizontally mirrored image. Flipping horizontally restores the correct, non-mirrored orientation.

### Preparing Photos for a Document or Print Layout

Once your photos are correctly oriented, you may want to combine several into a single file — FileCast's [JPG to PDF](/convert/jpg-to-pdf/) tool can bundle a batch of rotated photos into one PDF ready to print or share.

### Creating a Mirrored Graphic on Purpose

Design work sometimes calls for a deliberately mirrored version of an icon or illustration — for example, to face a different direction in a layout. Flipping handles this in one step without needing an image editor.
