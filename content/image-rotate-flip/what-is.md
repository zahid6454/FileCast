## What Is Image Rotate/Flip?

Rotating an image turns it in 90° steps — a common fix for photos that show up sideways or upside-down, often because a phone's orientation wasn't recorded correctly. Flipping mirrors an image horizontally or vertically, which is useful for correcting a mirrored scan or intentionally reversing a graphic.

Both operations change how the image is oriented without cropping, resizing, or otherwise altering its content — you get back the same picture, just turned or mirrored.

### Why Rotate or Flip an Image?

Photos taken on a phone held sideways, scans fed into a scanner the wrong way, or screenshots from a rotated display often need a quick orientation fix before they're usable. Flipping is less common but comes up when a design needs a mirrored version of a graphic, or when a scanned document came out reversed.

### Supported Formats

This tool accepts JPG, PNG, and WebP images. You can rotate in 90° increments (90°, 180°, or 270°) and flip horizontally or vertically — including both together in a single pass.

### How This Tool Works

This tool runs entirely in your browser using the HTML canvas. When you select an image, your device redraws it locally at the chosen rotation and flip — your file is never uploaded to any server. Pick your settings, then download the result.
