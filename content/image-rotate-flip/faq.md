## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to rotate or flip my images here?

Yes. This tool processes your image entirely in your browser. Your file is never uploaded to any server — everything happens locally on your device.

### Can I rotate and flip at the same time?

Yes. Choose a rotation and a flip direction together, and both are applied in a single pass — you don't need to run the tool twice.

### Why did my image's width and height swap?

Rotating by 90° or 270° turns the image on its side, so what was the width becomes the height and vice versa. This is expected and matches what you'd see if you physically rotated a printed photo. 180° rotation and any flip leave the dimensions unchanged.

### Does rotating or flipping reduce image quality?

There's no meaningful quality loss — the image is redrawn at the same resolution with only its orientation changed. JPEG output uses a high-quality setting, matching FileCast's other image tools.

### Which image formats are supported?

This tool accepts JPG, PNG, and WebP files. The output keeps the same format as your original, including transparency for PNG and WebP.
