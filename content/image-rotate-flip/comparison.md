## Rotate vs Flip — Which One Do You Need

Rotate and flip both change an image's orientation, but they solve different problems — mixing them up is a common source of confusion.

<tr><td class="feature-cell"><span class="feature-cell__inner"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-target"></use></svg>Common use</span></td><td data-label="Rotate">Fixing a sideways or upside-down photo</td><td data-label="Flip">Correcting a mirrored scan, or reversing a graphic on purpose</td></tr>
