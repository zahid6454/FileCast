## Rotate vs Flip — Which One Do You Need

Rotate and flip both change an image's orientation, but they solve different problems — mixing them up is a common source of confusion.

| Feature | Rotate | Flip |
|---|---|---|
| What it does | Turns the image around a center point | Mirrors the image across an axis |
| Common use | Fixing a sideways or upside-down photo | Correcting a mirrored scan, or reversing a graphic on purpose |
| Steps available | 90°, 180°, 270° | Horizontal or vertical |
| Text readability | Stays readable at 180°, sideways at 90°/270° | Text becomes mirrored (unreadable) unless the source was already mirrored |
| Dimensions | Width/height swap at 90°/270° | Width and height stay the same |

### Use Rotate When

- A photo displays sideways or upside-down
- A scanned document was fed into the scanner in the wrong orientation
- A screenshot was captured from a rotated display

### Use Flip When

- A scan or photo came out as a mirror image of the original
- You need a mirrored version of a logo or graphic for a specific layout
- You're correcting text or an image that reads backwards
