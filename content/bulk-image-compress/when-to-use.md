### Optimizing Images for a Website Launch

Launching a new site or adding a batch of pages means uploading many images at once — product photos, team headshots, blog illustrations, and banners. Compressing them all before upload ensures your pages load quickly from day one. Running 10 images through the bulk tool in one pass is far faster than handling each one separately.

### Preparing Product Listings

Online stores and marketplace listings rely on multiple photos per item. When you have several products to list, each with three to five images, the total file count adds up quickly. Bulk compression lets you process an entire set of product shots in a single session, keeping your listings consistent and your upload times short.

### Sharing Event or Travel Photos

After a trip, wedding, or company event, you often end up with a collection of photos to share with friends, family, or colleagues. Compressing a batch at once makes the whole set easier to email, upload to a shared album, or send through messaging apps — without the back-and-forth of doing one image at a time.

</div>

### Cleaning Up Cloud Storage

When your cloud drive or photo backup is creeping toward its limit, compressing older images you want to keep but don't need at full quality can reclaim a meaningful amount of space. Processing them in groups of 10 makes the cleanup much less tedious than working through your library file by file.

</div>

### Updating Blog or Portfolio Content

Refreshing existing content with better-optimized images improves load times for returning visitors. Pulling a batch of images from existing posts, compressing them, and re-uploading is a straightforward way to improve site performance without changing any design or layout.
