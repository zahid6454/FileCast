## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to compress my images here?

Yes. This tool processes your images entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server — everything happens locally on your device. No one else can see or access your images during or after compression, regardless of how many files you process in a session.

### How many images can I compress at once?

You can compress up to 10 images at once. Each file can be up to 10 MB in size. The tool processes your files sequentially, so you'll see each result appear one after another as the batch progresses.

### Do all images get the same quality setting?

Yes. The quality level you choose applies to every image in the batch. If you have a mix of photos where some need heavier compression and others need lighter treatment, you may want to split them into separate batches or use the single-file compressor for the ones that need individual attention.

### Which image formats are supported?

This tool accepts JPG, PNG, and WebP files. You can mix formats in a single batch — for example, five JPGs and three PNGs together. Each compressed file keeps its original format.

### What if I have more than 10 images?

The tool handles up to 10 images per session. If you have a larger collection, process the first batch, download your results, then start a new session with the next group. Each session is independent, so there's no limit on how many batches you can run back to back.
