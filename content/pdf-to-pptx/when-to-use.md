### Turning a PDF Report into a Presentation

You have a finished report as a PDF and need to walk a room through it. Converting to PPTX gives you a slide per page you can present with, add a title slide to, or drop into a larger meeting deck — without rebuilding it in PowerPoint from scratch.

### Repurposing a Printed Handout as Slides

A handout or one-pager exists only as a PDF, but you need to present the same content on screen. Converting to PPTX turns each page into a slide you can click through in presenter view, and adds real slide transitions a static PDF viewer can't offer.

### Pulling Pages Out of a Larger PDF for a Focused Deck

You only need a handful of pages from a longer PDF for one specific presentation. Extract those pages with the [PDF Extract Pages](/convert/pdf-extract-pages/) tool first, then convert the shorter PDF to PPTX for a lean, focused deck instead of a full-length one.

### Building on Top of an Exported Deck

Some tools only export presentations as PDF. Converting back to PPTX lets you reopen the deck in PowerPoint, add new slides, reorder existing ones, or merge it into another presentation.
