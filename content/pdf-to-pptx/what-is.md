## What Is PDF to PowerPoint Conversion?

PDF to PowerPoint conversion turns each page of a PDF into its own slide in a genuine PPTX presentation — one you can open in PowerPoint, Google Slides, or Keynote, reorder, and present directly, instead of a static document.

PDFs are common for finished decks and printed handouts, but they aren't presentable or reorderable the way a slide deck is. When you only have the PDF version of a presentation — or want to turn a PDF report into slides for a meeting — converting to PPTX gets you there without rebuilding it from scratch.

### What Gets Preserved?

Every page's exact visual appearance — layout, colors, images, and text positioning — carries over precisely, since each slide is built from a high-resolution rendering of its source page. That also means the text on each slide isn't separately editable the way text typed directly into PowerPoint is; you can add new slides, reorder existing ones, and present the deck as-is, but changing the words on a converted slide means editing the original PDF and reconverting.
