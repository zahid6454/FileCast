## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my PDF here?

Yes. Your file is encrypted during upload, converted on our server, and deleted immediately after the conversion completes. We never store, log, or read the content of your documents. No account is required and no conversion history is kept.

### Can I edit the text on the converted slides?

Not the text that was already on the PDF page. Each slide is a high-resolution image of that page, so the layout and text look exactly right, but the words themselves aren't a separate, editable text box the way text typed directly into PowerPoint is. You can freely add new slides, reorder slides, delete slides, or present the deck as-is — you just can't click into the converted content and retype a word.

### Will the slide size and layout look right?

Yes. Slides are sized to match your PDF's own aspect ratio, so a landscape PDF produces a landscape deck and a portrait PDF produces a portrait deck, with the full page visible on every slide.

### Is there a file size limit?

Yes, the maximum file size is 25MB. Most PDF documents are well under this limit. If your file exceeds it, try splitting it into smaller sections using the PDF Split tool, converting each part separately.

### Can I convert multiple PDFs at once?

Currently, this tool converts one file at a time. Upload your PDF, download the presentation, then start another conversion for the next file.
