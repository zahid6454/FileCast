## Common Scenarios for Rotating PDF Pages

### Fixing Scanned Documents

Scanners don't always detect page orientation correctly, especially with mixed portrait and landscape pages. A contract scanned in a batch might have a few pages turned sideways. Rotating those pages puts the entire document in the right reading direction without re-scanning the whole stack.

### Correcting Landscape Tables and Charts

Reports and presentations sometimes include landscape-oriented pages for wide tables, charts, or diagrams. If those pages end up rotated the wrong way when exported to PDF, a quick turn sets them straight so readers don't have to tilt their screens.

### Preparing Files for Printing

Printers rely on correct page orientation to produce readable output. A sideways page will print sideways, wasting paper and time. Rotating before printing ensures every page comes out facing the right way, especially important for double-sided documents where alignment matters.

### Cleaning Up Mobile Scans

Phone scanning apps are convenient but not always accurate with orientation. Photos of receipts, whiteboards, or handwritten notes often end up upside down or rotated 90 degrees. Fixing the orientation makes these scans usable for filing, sharing, or attaching to expense reports.

### Standardizing Multi-Source Documents

When assembling a PDF from multiple sources — different scanners, different people, different software — you often end up with inconsistent page orientations. Rotating the misaligned pages brings the whole document into a uniform, professional-looking format before you share it.
