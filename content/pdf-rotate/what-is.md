## What Is PDF Rotate?

PDF rotate is the process of turning one or more pages in a PDF document by 90, 180, or 270 degrees. This fixes pages that appear sideways or upside down — a common problem with scanned documents, photos saved as PDFs, and files received from others. The content on each page stays exactly the same; only the orientation changes.

Misoriented pages make documents frustrating to read. You end up tilting your head, rotating your screen, or printing pages just to read them right-side up. A quick rotation saves that hassle and produces a clean, properly oriented file.

### Why Rotate Instead of Re-scanning?

Re-scanning a document takes time and requires access to the original paper copy and a scanner. If the content is already captured in the PDF, rotation gets the job done in seconds without any hardware. The text, images, and formatting are all preserved — the page simply faces the right direction.

Rotation is also more reliable than re-scanning when it comes to consistency. Each scan can introduce slight variations in alignment, brightness, or cropping. Rotating the existing page avoids those inconsistencies entirely.
