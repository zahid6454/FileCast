## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to rotate my PDF here?

Yes. This tool processes your file entirely in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to any server — everything happens locally on your device. No one else can see or access your document during or after the rotation.

### Does rotating affect the quality of my pages?

No. Rotation changes the orientation of the page without altering the content in any way. Text, images, and formatting remain identical — the page simply faces a different direction. There is no quality loss or re-compression involved.

### Can I rotate individual pages within a document?

Yes. You can select specific pages to rotate while leaving the rest unchanged. This is useful when a document has a mix of correctly and incorrectly oriented pages — just fix the ones that need it.

### What rotation angles are available?

You can rotate pages by 90 degrees clockwise, 180 degrees (flipping upside down), or 270 degrees clockwise (which is the same as 90 degrees counter-clockwise). These three options cover every common orientation problem.

### Can I rotate all pages in a document at once?

Yes. If every page in your PDF needs the same rotation — like a document that was scanned entirely sideways — you can apply the rotation to all pages in one step rather than adjusting each page individually.
