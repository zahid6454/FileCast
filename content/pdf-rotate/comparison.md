## Rotating vs Re-scanning — When to Rotate

Sometimes a misoriented page is best fixed by rotating the existing PDF. Other times, re-scanning the original is the better call. Here's how to decide.

      <h3>Re-scan When</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>The original scan is blurry, cropped, or too dark to use</li>

      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need a higher resolution than the existing PDF provides</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>The paper original has been updated since the last scan</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Multiple pages need both re-orientation and quality improvement</li>

      <span class="panel__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-refresh"></use></svg></span>
      <h3>Rotate When</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>The page content is clear and well-captured, just facing the wrong direction</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You don't have access to the original paper document</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need a quick fix without any hardware</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>The document was created digitally but exported in the wrong orientation</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You received a file from someone else and can't ask them to resend it</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>A few pages in a larger document are sideways while the rest are correct</li>
    </ul>
