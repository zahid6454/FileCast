## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to merge my PDFs here?

Yes. This tool processes your files entirely in your browser. Your PDFs are never uploaded to any server — everything happens locally on your device. No one else can see or access your documents during or after the merge.

### Does merging change the content of my files?

No. Each PDF is added to the combined document exactly as it is. The text, images, formatting, and page layouts of every file are preserved. The only change is that they now live in a single document instead of separate ones.

### Can I control the order of the merged pages?

Yes. After adding your files, you can drag them into whatever order you prefer before merging. The final PDF will follow the sequence you set — first file's pages appear first, followed by the second file's pages, and so on.

### What if one of my PDFs is password-protected?

Password-protected PDFs need to be unlocked before they can be merged. If a file is encrypted, the tool won't be able to read its contents. You'll need to remove the password using the original software that created it, then add the unlocked version.

### Can I merge multiple files at once?

Yes. You can add up to 10 files at once and combine them into a single PDF. Simply drop all your files onto the page, arrange them in the order you want, and merge them in one step.
