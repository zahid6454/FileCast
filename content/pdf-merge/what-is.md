## What Is PDF Merge?

PDF merge is the process of combining two or more separate PDF files into a single document. Instead of sending or storing a handful of related files, you get one unified PDF that keeps everything together in the order you choose. The original formatting, page layouts, and content of each file are preserved exactly as they were.

This is especially useful when you've collected materials from different sources — a cover letter, a resume, and a reference list, for example — and need to submit them as one file. Merging avoids the confusion of multiple attachments and ensures the recipient sees everything in the right sequence.

### Why Combine PDFs?

Keeping related documents in separate files creates friction. Recipients have to open each one individually, and it's easy for a file to go missing or get opened out of order. A single merged PDF eliminates those problems. It's also cleaner for archiving — one file per project or application is far easier to organize than a scattered collection of parts.

Many online portals, school submission systems, and government forms only accept a single PDF upload. If your materials are spread across several files, merging is the only way to get everything submitted in one go.

### How This Tool Works

This tool runs entirely in your browser. When you add your PDF files, your device reads each one and assembles them into a combined document — all locally, in your device's memory. Your files are never uploaded to any server. You arrange the order by dragging files into position, and the merged PDF is ready to download in seconds.
