## What Is WebP?

WebP is an image format created by Google. It was designed to make images smaller without making them look worse, which helps websites load faster.

Today, most websites use WebP for their photos and graphics. When you save an image from a website, download a product photo, or grab a screenshot from an online article, there's a good chance the file you get is in WebP format.

### The Problem with WebP

Even though WebP works great on the web, it doesn't work everywhere else. Many common apps and devices still can't open WebP files:

- Older photo editing apps may not recognize the format
- Some phones and tablets can't display WebP images
- Printing services often require JPG or PNG files
- Social media platforms and online forms sometimes reject WebP uploads
- Email attachments in WebP may not preview correctly for the person receiving them
