## WebP vs JPG — When to Use Each

Both WebP and JPG are popular image formats, but they have different strengths. Here's how they compare:

      <span class="panel__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-photo"></use></svg></span>
      <h3>Keep WebP When</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You're using the image on a website or blog</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>File size matters and you want the smallest file possible</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need transparency in a compressed image</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>The image will stay on your own computer and your apps can open it</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You're working with a platform that accepts WebP</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="panel panel--accent">
    <div class="panel__head">
      <span class="panel__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-arrow-convert"></use></svg></span>
      <h3>Convert to JPG When</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need to share the image with others and want it to open anywhere</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You're uploading to a site or form that doesn't accept WebP</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You want to edit the image in a photo app that doesn't support WebP</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need to print the image through an online printing service</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You're attaching the image to an email and want it to display correctly</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You're saving photos for long-term storage in a universal format</li>
    </ul>
