## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my images here?

Yes. This tool converts your files entirely inside your browser. Your images are never uploaded to a server — everything happens on your own device. No one else can see or access your files during the conversion.

### Why can't I open WebP files on my computer?

WebP is a newer image format that not all software supports yet. Older operating systems, photo viewers, and editing apps were built before WebP existed, so they don't recognize the file type. Converting to JPG gives you a file that works with virtually any app or device.

### Does the image quality change when I convert?

There may be a very small change in quality, but for most everyday uses — sharing, printing, uploading — you won't notice any difference. The converted JPG will look the same as the original to the human eye.

### Will the file size increase after conversion?

It can, yes. WebP files are typically smaller than JPG files because WebP uses a more efficient way of compressing images. After converting, the JPG version may be somewhat larger, but it will be compatible with far more apps and devices.

### Can I convert multiple files at once?

Currently, this tool converts one file at a time. Drop a file, convert it, download, then click "Convert Another" for the next one. Batch conversion is planned for a future update.
