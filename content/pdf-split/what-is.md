## What Is PDF Split?

PDF split is the process of extracting specific pages from a PDF and saving them as separate files. Instead of sharing or storing an entire document when you only need a few pages, you can pull out exactly the ones that matter. The original file stays untouched — you're creating new, smaller PDFs from selected pages.

This comes in handy when a long document contains sections meant for different people, or when you only need a portion of a report, contract, or manual. Rather than asking someone to scroll to page 47, you hand them a clean file with just the pages they need.

### Why Split a PDF?

Large PDFs can be cumbersome. They're slow to open, hard to navigate, and impractical to share when the recipient only cares about a few pages. Splitting lets you break a bulky document into focused pieces — a single chapter, a specific form, or a set of pages for review.

It's also useful for privacy. If a document contains sensitive information on some pages but not others, splitting lets you share only the non-sensitive portions without exposing the rest.

### How This Tool Works

This tool runs entirely in your browser. When you drop a PDF onto the page, your device reads the file and lets you select which pages to extract — all locally, in your device's memory. Your file is never uploaded to any server. Choose your pages, and the extracted PDF is ready to download in seconds.
