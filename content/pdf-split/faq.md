## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to split my PDF here?

Yes. This tool processes your file entirely in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to any server — everything happens locally on your device. No one else can see or access your document during or after the split.

### Does splitting change the original file?

No. The original PDF remains completely untouched. Splitting creates a new, separate file containing only the pages you selected. Your source document stays exactly as it was before.

### Can I extract non-consecutive pages?

Yes. You can select any combination of pages — whether they're next to each other or scattered throughout the document. The extracted file will include only the pages you chose, in the order they appeared in the original.

### What happens to links and bookmarks in the split file?

Internal links that point to pages within the extracted section will continue to work. Links that reference pages outside your selection may no longer function since those pages aren't included in the new file. Bookmarks behave the same way — only those pointing to extracted pages are preserved.

### Can I split a large PDF into multiple parts at once?

Currently, this tool extracts one set of selected pages at a time. Choose your pages, download the result, then start another extraction for a different set. This gives you full control over exactly which pages end up in each file.
