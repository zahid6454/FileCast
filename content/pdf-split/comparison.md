## Splitting vs Keeping Whole — When to Separate Pages

Not every large PDF needs to be broken apart. Sometimes the full document is exactly what you need. Here's how to decide.

      <h3>Split When</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You only need to share a specific section, like a single chapter or form</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>The file is too large to attach in an email or upload to a portal</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Certain pages contain confidential information you don't want to distribute</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Different team members need different parts of the same document</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You want to archive individual sections for easier searching later</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>A recipient has asked for just a few pages rather than the whole thing</li>
    </ul>
