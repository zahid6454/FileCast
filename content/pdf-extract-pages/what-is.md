## What Is PDF Extract Pages?

This tool pulls the pages you specify out of a PDF and saves them as a brand-new, separate PDF — the pages you list, in the order you list them. The original file isn't modified; you get a new file containing just the pages you chose.

It's different from splitting a PDF into individual single-page files: extraction lets you pick any combination of pages — a range, scattered individual pages, or both — and combines them into one new document.

### Why Extract Pages from a PDF?

Large documents often contain sections that are only relevant to a subset of readers — an appendix, a specific exhibit, a chapter someone asked for. Extracting just those pages means you can share exactly what's needed without sending the entire document.

It's also useful for pulling reference material: quotes, tables, or figures scattered across a longer report that you want gathered into a smaller, standalone file.

### How This Tool Works

This tool runs entirely in your browser. When you drop a PDF onto the page and list which pages to extract, your device builds a new PDF from just those pages locally, in your device's memory. Your file is never uploaded to any server. The extracted PDF is ready to download in seconds.
