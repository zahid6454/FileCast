## Common Scenarios for Extracting PDF Pages

<div class="scenario-list" markdown="1">
<div class="scenario" markdown="1">
<div class="scenario__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-file-plus"></use></svg></div>
<div class="scenario__body" markdown="1">

### Pulling Out a Single Chapter or Section

A long report or manual often has just one section relevant to a particular reader. Extracting that section into its own file means you can share it directly, without sending — or asking someone to navigate through — the entire document.

</div>
</div>
<div class="scenario" markdown="1">
<div class="scenario__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-page"></use></svg></div>
<div class="scenario__body" markdown="1">

### Sharing a Specific Exhibit or Appendix

Legal and contractual documents often bundle exhibits or appendices with the main body. Extracting just the exhibit someone needs saves them from digging through unrelated pages, and keeps what you share focused.

</div>
</div>
<div class="scenario" markdown="1">
<div class="scenario__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-search"></use></svg></div>
<div class="scenario__body" markdown="1">

### Building a Reference Packet from Multiple Places in a Document

If the pages you need are scattered — a summary on page 2, a table on page 15, a conclusion on page 40 — extraction lets you pull all three into a single new file in whatever order makes sense for the reference packet. Before sharing that packet externally, our [Hash Generator](/convert/hash-generator/) can produce a checksum so recipients can confirm they received exactly the file you sent.

</div>
</div>

<div class="scenario__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-shield"></use></svg></div>
<div class="scenario__body" markdown="1">

### Creating a Redacted Public Version

If certain pages of a document are sensitive but others are fine to share publicly, extracting the shareable pages into a new file keeps the sensitive content entirely out of the file that goes out, rather than relying on hiding or redacting it in place.

</div>
</div>
<div class="scenario" markdown="1">
<div class="scenario__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-edit"></use></svg></div>
<div class="scenario__body" markdown="1">

### Isolating Pages for Further Editing

If you only need to touch up or rework a small part of a larger PDF, extracting just those pages first — editing them separately, then merging them back in — is often simpler than working inside the full document.
