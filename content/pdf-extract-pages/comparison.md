## Extracting Pages vs Splitting or Removing Pages

There are three related but different ways to change which pages end up in a PDF. Here's how they compare.

<div class="table-scroll">
<table class="icon-table">
<colgroup><col><col><col><col></colgroup>
<thead><tr><th>Consideration</th><th>Extract Pages</th><th>PDF Split</th><th>PDF Remove Pages</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td class="feature-cell"><span class="feature-cell__inner"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-page"></use></svg>Output</span></td><td data-label="Extract Pages">One new PDF with just the pages you list</td><td data-label="PDF Split">One file per page</td><td data-label="PDF Remove Pages">The original pages, minus the ones you list</td></tr>
<tr><td class="feature-cell"><span class="feature-cell__inner"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-target"></use></svg>Best for</span></td><td data-label="Extract Pages">Pulling a specific selection into one file</td><td data-label="PDF Split">Breaking a document apart entirely</td><td data-label="PDF Remove Pages">Deleting a few unwanted pages, keeping the rest</td></tr>
<tr><td class="feature-cell"><span class="feature-cell__inner"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-sliders"></use></svg>Control over page order in the output</span></td><td data-label="Extract Pages">Yes — set the order in your list</td><td data-label="PDF Split">Not applicable, each page is separate</td><td data-label="PDF Remove Pages">No — remaining pages keep their original order</td></tr>
<tr><td class="feature-cell"><span class="feature-cell__inner"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-chevron-right"></use></svg>Typical use</span></td><td data-label="Extract Pages">"I need pages 3, 7, and 10-12 as one file"</td><td data-label="PDF Split">"I need every page as its own file"</td><td data-label="PDF Remove Pages">"I need everything except pages 4 and 9"</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>
