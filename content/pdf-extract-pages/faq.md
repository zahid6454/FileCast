## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to extract pages from my PDF here?

Yes. This tool processes your file entirely in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to any server — everything happens locally on your device.

### How do I specify which pages to extract?

List page numbers and ranges separated by commas — for example, "1-3,5,8-10" extracts pages 1 through 3, page 5, and pages 8 through 10, all combined into one new PDF.

### Can I change the order of the extracted pages?

Yes. Pages come out in the order you list them, not necessarily their original order — listing "5,1,3" produces a new PDF with page 5 first, then page 1, then page 3.

### Does extracting pages affect my original file?

No. This tool creates a brand-new PDF containing the pages you chose. Your original file is untouched.

### What happens if I list a page number that doesn't exist?

The tool tells you which page number is out of range for the PDF you uploaded and doesn't produce a file, so you can correct the list and try again.
