## What Is XLSX?

XLSX is the default file format for Microsoft Excel spreadsheets. Like DOCX, it uses an XML-based structure inside a ZIP container and was introduced with Office 2007. XLSX files can contain multiple worksheets, formulas, charts, pivot tables, and formatting — making them the standard for data organization and financial reporting.

### Why Convert XLSX to PDF?

Spreadsheets are designed for calculation and data manipulation, but they're difficult to share reliably. Column widths, page breaks, and print areas often look different across Excel versions, Google Sheets, and other spreadsheet programs. Converting to PDF freezes the layout exactly as you see it, creating a document that prints correctly and displays consistently on any device.
