## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my spreadsheet here?

Yes. Your file is encrypted during upload, converted on our server, and deleted immediately after the conversion completes. We never store, log, or read the content of your spreadsheets. No account is required and no conversion history is kept.

### Will all worksheets be included in the PDF?

The conversion processes all visible worksheets in your workbook. Each sheet typically becomes a separate section in the resulting PDF. Hidden sheets are not included.

### Are charts and images preserved?

Yes. Charts, images, and conditional formatting are rendered in the PDF as they appear in the spreadsheet. The conversion engine captures the visual output, not just the raw data.

### Is there a file size limit?

Yes, the maximum file size is 25MB. Most Excel files are well under this limit. If your file is larger, try removing unused worksheets or reducing embedded image sizes before converting.

### Can I convert multiple spreadsheets at once?

Currently, this tool converts one file at a time. Upload your spreadsheet, download the result, then start another conversion for the next file.
