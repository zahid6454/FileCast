## XLSX vs PDF — When to Use Each

<div class="table-scroll">
<table class="icon-table">
<colgroup><col><col><col></colgroup>
<thead><tr><th>Feature</th><th>XLSX</th><th>PDF</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td class="feature-cell"><span class="feature-cell__inner"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-edit"></use></svg>Editing</span></td><td data-label="XLSX">Fully editable, formulas active</td><td data-label="PDF">View-only by design</td></tr>
<tr><td class="feature-cell"><span class="feature-cell__inner"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-search"></use></svg>Data analysis</span></td><td data-label="XLSX">Sorting, filtering, pivot tables</td><td data-label="PDF">Static snapshot</td></tr>
<tr><td class="feature-cell"><span class="feature-cell__inner"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-maximize"></use></svg>Layout consistency</span></td><td data-label="XLSX">Varies by screen size and software</td><td data-label="PDF">Fixed layout on all devices</td></tr>
<tr><td class="feature-cell"><span class="feature-cell__inner"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-printer"></use></svg>Printing</span></td><td data-label="XLSX">Page breaks often unpredictable</td><td data-label="PDF">Print-ready, WYSIWYG</td></tr>
<tr><td class="feature-cell"><span class="feature-cell__inner"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-minimize"></use></svg>File size</span></td><td data-label="XLSX">Compact for data-heavy files</td><td data-label="PDF">Can be larger with many pages</td></tr>
<tr><td class="feature-cell"><span class="feature-cell__inner"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-user"></use></svg>Collaboration</span></td><td data-label="XLSX">Real-time co-editing</td><td data-label="PDF">Read-only sharing</td></tr>
<tr><td class="feature-cell"><span class="feature-cell__inner"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-code"></use></svg>Formulas</span></td><td data-label="XLSX">Live calculations</td><td data-label="PDF">Values only (no formulas)</td></tr>

<div class="panel-grid">
  <div class="panel panel--neutral">
    <div class="panel__head">
      <span class="panel__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-code"></use></svg></span>
      <h3>Keep XLSX When</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You're actively working with the data — calculating, sorting, filtering, or building charts</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You're collaborating with others who need to edit the numbers too</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>The spreadsheet isn't final — PDF loses all formula and editing functionality</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="panel panel--accent">
    <div class="panel__head">
      <span class="panel__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-page"></use></svg></span>
      <h3>Convert to PDF When</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>The spreadsheet is ready to share as a final report — a financial statement, budget summary, or client data export</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need a print-ready table with a fixed layout, not something that shifts by screen size</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>The recipient shouldn't be able to modify the numbers</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>
