## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to flatten my PDF here?

Yes. This tool processes your file entirely in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to any server — everything happens locally on your device.

### What happens to filled-in form data after flattening?

It stays visible, exactly as it appeared before flattening — it's just no longer stored as an editable form field. The values are baked directly into the page content instead.

### Can I un-flatten a PDF afterward?

No. Flattening is a one-way conversion — the interactive form fields are gone once converted to static content. If you might need the editable form again, keep your original file before flattening.

### What if my PDF doesn't have any form fields?

Nothing changes. Flattening only affects form fields and annotations, so a PDF that doesn't have any comes through unchanged.

### Does flattening affect the visual appearance of the document?

No. The document looks and prints exactly the same before and after — flattening only changes whether the form fields remain interactive, not how anything is displayed.
