## What Is Flatten PDF?

Flattening a PDF converts its interactive form fields and annotations — text boxes, checkboxes, dropdowns, signatures, comments — into permanent, static page content. Whatever was filled in or drawn stays visible exactly as it looked, but it's no longer editable, and the form fields themselves stop functioning as fields.

Once flattened, the PDF looks and prints identically, but it behaves like a plain document rather than a fillable form.

### Why Flatten a PDF?

A fillable form is useful while it's being filled out, but once it's complete, leaving the fields editable creates risk — anyone who opens the finished form could accidentally (or deliberately) change an answer. Flattening locks the filled-in values in place, so what was submitted is what stays visible from then on.

It's also useful for consistency: a flattened PDF renders identically across every viewer, since it no longer depends on how that viewer chooses to display interactive form fields.
