## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my images here?

Yes. This tool processes your images entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server — everything happens locally on your device. No one else can see or access your images during or after conversion.

### What happens to transparency when I convert PNG to JPG?

JPG does not support transparency. Any transparent areas in your PNG will be filled with a solid white background in the converted JPG file. If you need to keep transparency, stick with the PNG format.

### Does the image quality change?

There is a very slight reduction in quality because JPG uses a different type of compression than PNG. For photographs and everyday images, the difference is virtually impossible to notice. For graphics with sharp text or fine lines, you may see minor softening around edges.

### Will the file size decrease?

In most cases, yes — significantly. JPG files are typically 50-80% smaller than the same image in PNG format. The exact savings depend on the image content: photographs see the biggest reductions, while simple graphics with few colors may see smaller but still meaningful savings.

### Can I convert multiple files at once?

Currently, this tool converts one file at a time. Drop a file, convert it, download, then click "Convert Another" for the next one. Batch conversion is planned for a future update.
