## Common Scenarios for PNG to JPG Conversion

<div class="scenario-list" markdown="1">
<div class="scenario" markdown="1">

<div class="scenario__body" markdown="1">

### Reducing File Size for Email

Email services typically limit attachment sizes to 10-25 MB. A handful of high-resolution PNG photos can hit that ceiling quickly. Converting to JPG can shrink each file by 50-80%, letting you attach more images in a single email without bouncing.

</div>
</div>

<div class="scenario__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-upload-cloud"></use></svg></div>
<div class="scenario__body" markdown="1">

### Uploading to Websites That Require JPG

Many online forms, job applications, and government portals specifically ask for JPG images. If your screenshots or scanned documents are saved as PNG, a quick conversion gets them into the right format without any hassle.

</div>
</div>
<div class="scenario" markdown="1">
<div class="scenario__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-user"></use></svg></div>
<div class="scenario__body" markdown="1">

### Posting on Social Media

Platforms like Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn handle JPG images most reliably. While they accept PNG, they'll often re-compress your image on their end anyway. Starting with a well-optimized JPG gives you more control over how your image looks when it's posted.

</div>
</div>

<div class="scenario__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-minimize"></use></svg></div>
<div class="scenario__body" markdown="1">

### Saving Storage Space

If you have hundreds of PNG screenshots or photos taking up space on your phone or computer, converting them to JPG can free up a significant amount of storage. This is especially useful on devices with limited space, like older phones or laptops with small drives.

</div>
</div>

### Photo Printing

Most online photo printing services prefer JPG files. Print labs are optimized for this format, and uploading JPG ensures your colors and quality are handled correctly throughout the printing process. If your photos are saved as PNG, converting before you upload avoids any format-related surprises at the printer.
