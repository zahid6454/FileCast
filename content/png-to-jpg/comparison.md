## PNG vs JPG — When to Use Each

Both PNG and JPG are image formats you'll encounter every day, but they're designed for different purposes. Here's how they compare side by side.

| Feature | PNG | JPG |
|---|---|---|
| File size | Larger | Much smaller |
| Transparency | Supported | Not supported |
| Image quality | Lossless (pixel-perfect) | Slight quality loss (usually unnoticeable in photos) |
| Best for | Logos, icons, screenshots, graphics with text | Photos, web images, email attachments |
| Compatibility | Widely supported | Universal — works everywhere |

### Keep PNG When

- Your image has a transparent background you need to preserve
- You're working with logos, icons, or graphics with sharp text
- You need to edit the image multiple times without any quality loss
- You're creating digital art or illustrations with flat colors and hard edges
- The image will be used in design software for further editing

### Convert to JPG When

- You're sharing photos by email or messaging apps and want smaller files
- A website or online form specifically requires JPG uploads
- You need to save storage space on your device
- You're uploading images to social media platforms
- The image is a photograph without any transparent areas
- You're preparing images for printing through an online service
