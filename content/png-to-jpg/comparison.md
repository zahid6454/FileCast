## PNG vs JPG — When to Use Each

Both PNG and JPG are image formats you'll encounter every day, but they're designed for different purposes. Here's how they compare side by side.

      <span class="panel__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-photo"></use></svg></span>
      <h3>Keep PNG When</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Your image has a transparent background you need to preserve</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You're working with logos, icons, or graphics with sharp text</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need to edit the image multiple times without any quality loss</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You're creating digital art or illustrations with flat colors and hard edges</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>The image will be used in design software for further editing</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

    <div class="panel__head">
      <span class="panel__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-arrow-convert"></use></svg></span>
      <h3>Convert to JPG When</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You're sharing photos by email or messaging apps and want smaller files</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>A website or online form specifically requires JPG uploads</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need to save storage space on your device</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You're uploading images to social media platforms</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>The image is a photograph without any transparent areas</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You're preparing images for printing through an online service</li>
    </ul>
