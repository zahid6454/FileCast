## What Is PNG?

PNG (Portable Network Graphics) is a popular image format known for its high quality and support for transparent backgrounds. It's the go-to choice for logos, icons, screenshots, and any image where you need crisp edges or see-through areas. Because PNG uses lossless compression, every pixel in the original image is preserved exactly as-is.

The trade-off for that quality is file size. A PNG photo can be several times larger than the same image saved as a JPG. For everyday photos, web uploads, and email attachments, that extra size adds up fast without a noticeable difference in how the picture looks.

### Why Convert to JPG?

JPG is the most widely accepted image format on the planet. Nearly every website, app, email client, and device handles JPG without any issues. It achieves much smaller file sizes by slightly reducing image detail in ways that are usually invisible to the eye — especially in photographs.

Converting from PNG to JPG makes sense when you don't need transparency and want a smaller, more compatible file. Social media platforms, online forms, and photo printing services all work best with JPG.

### How This Tool Works

This converter runs entirely in your browser. When you drop a PNG file onto the page, your device processes the image and produces a JPG version — all locally, in your device's memory. Your image is never uploaded to any server. The conversion typically takes under a second, even for large files, and you can download the result immediately.
