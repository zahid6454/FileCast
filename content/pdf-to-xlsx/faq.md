## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my PDF here?

Yes. Your file is encrypted during upload, converted on our server, and deleted immediately after the conversion completes. We never store, log, or read the content of your documents. No account is required and no conversion history is kept.

### Will it extract every table correctly?

PDFs with real, ruled tables — invoices, statements, price lists, exported reports — extract into genuine spreadsheet cells with high accuracy. Pages without a clearly detected table still convert, but come across as plain text lines rather than columns, since there's no table structure to reconstruct.

### Can I convert scanned PDFs?

This tool works best with PDFs that contain real text and table structure (created from software, exported from a spreadsheet, or digitally generated). Scanned PDFs — where pages are essentially images — don't have an extractable text layer, so their content can't be pulled into cells.

### Is there a file size limit?

Yes, the maximum file size is 25MB. Most PDF documents are well under this limit. If your file exceeds it, try splitting it into smaller sections using the PDF Split tool, converting each part separately.

### Can I convert multiple PDFs at once?

Currently, this tool converts one file at a time. Upload your PDF, download the spreadsheet, then start another conversion for the next file.
