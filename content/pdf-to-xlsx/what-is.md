## What Is PDF to Excel Conversion?

PDF to Excel conversion pulls tables and text out of a fixed-layout PDF and rebuilds them as real spreadsheet cells you can sort, filter, and run formulas on — instead of a flat image or a wall of unstructured text.

PDFs are built to look the same everywhere, not to be recalculated. When the numbers you need are locked inside a PDF — a bank statement, an invoice, a report someone exported — converting to Excel is the fastest way to get them back into a working spreadsheet.

### What Gets Extracted?

PDFs with real, ruled tables (invoices, statements, price lists, exported reports) convert best — each table becomes its own sheet, one row per table row, in real cells. Pages without a detected table still convert: their text comes across as one line per row on that sheet, so nothing is silently dropped, even if it isn't structured into columns. Scanned PDFs — pages that are just images of text, with no real text layer underneath — can't be extracted, since there's no text or table structure to read.
