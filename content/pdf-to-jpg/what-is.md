## What Is PDF to JPG Conversion?

PDF to JPG conversion turns each page of a PDF document into a separate JPG image file. JPG is the most widely supported image format — it opens on every device, uploads to any platform, and keeps file sizes small through efficient compression.

This is useful when you need to share PDF content in places that don't accept PDFs — social media, messaging apps, presentation slides, or websites. A JPG image works almost everywhere, while a PDF often doesn't.

### Why JPG Instead of PNG?

JPG produces smaller files than PNG, making it better for sharing and uploading. The trade-off is that JPG uses lossy compression, so there's a slight quality reduction — but for document pages, the difference is barely noticeable. Choose JPG when file size matters, PNG when you need pixel-perfect sharpness.

### How This Tool Works

This tool runs entirely in your browser. When you drop a PDF onto the page, your device renders each page at high resolution and converts it to a JPG image — all locally. Your file is never uploaded to any server. Each page becomes its own JPG file, ready to download in seconds.
