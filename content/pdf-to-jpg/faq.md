## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my PDF here?

Yes. This tool processes your file entirely in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to any server — everything happens locally on your device. No one else can see or access your document during or after the conversion.

### What quality will the JPG images be?

Pages are rendered at high resolution and saved as JPG with 92% quality — a setting that balances sharp text with reasonable file size. The result is suitable for presentations, web use, and printing.

### Can I still search or copy text from the JPG?

No. Once a PDF page becomes a JPG image, the text is part of the picture. You won't be able to select, search, or copy text from the resulting file. If you need searchable text, keep the original PDF alongside the images.

### What's the difference between PDF to JPG and PDF to PNG?

JPG produces smaller files but uses lossy compression — there's a slight quality reduction. PNG produces larger files but preserves every pixel perfectly. For most document pages, JPG looks identical to PNG at a fraction of the file size. Use PNG only when you need transparency or pixel-perfect sharpness.

### Can I convert a multi-page PDF all at once?

Yes. When you drop a multi-page PDF onto the page, every page is converted into its own separate JPG image. You can download each page individually, giving you full control over which pages you keep and use.
