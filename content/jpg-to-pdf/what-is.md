## What Is JPG to PDF Conversion?

JPG to PDF conversion takes one or more JPG photos and packages them into a single PDF document. Instead of a folder of loose photo files, you get one organized document that's easy to share, print, and archive.

This is especially useful for camera photos and phone screenshots — the two most common sources of JPG files — when you need to present them as a formal document rather than a scattered set of images.

### Why Convert JPGs to PDF?

JPGs are great for viewing, but they don't page through like a document. Sending ten separate photos is messier than sending one PDF, and many places that accept uploads expect a PDF, not a batch of image files.

Combining photos into a PDF also locks in their order. A PDF's pages stay in the sequence you set, while a batch of individually attached images can arrive shuffled or out of order.

### How This Tool Works

This tool runs entirely in your browser. When you add your JPG photos, your device assembles them into a PDF document — all locally, in your device's memory. Your files are never uploaded to any server. Arrange the photos in the order you want, and the finished PDF is ready to download in seconds.
