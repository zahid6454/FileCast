## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my JPGs here?

Yes. This tool processes your photos entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server — everything happens locally on your device. No one else can see or access your images during or after conversion.

### Does this tool only accept JPG files?

Yes — this tool is dedicated to JPG and JPEG files specifically. If you have a mix of formats, including PNG or WebP, use the general [Image to PDF](/convert/image-to-pdf/) converter instead.

### Does the photo quality change?

No. Your photos are placed into the PDF at their original resolution and quality. The tool does not compress or resize your images — what you see in the original JPG is what you get in the PDF.

### Can I control the page order?

Yes. Photos are added to the PDF in the order you select them. If you need a specific arrangement, add your files in the order you want them to appear — the first photo you add becomes the first page.

### How many photos can I convert at once?

You can add up to 10 JPG photos at once, with each file up to 20 MB in size. The photos are processed sequentially in your browser to create a single PDF containing all of them as separate pages.
