## What Is Image Resizing?

Image resizing changes the width and height of a photo or graphic to new dimensions. Unlike compression, which only reduces file size, resizing physically changes how large or small the image is — making it fit a specific space, meet dimension requirements, or simply take up less room on screen and on disk.

Resizing is one of the most common image tasks. Whether you need a profile picture that fits a 400x400 pixel square, a banner image at a specific width, or a photo scaled down for faster sharing, adjusting dimensions is the most direct way to get an image to the size you actually need.

### Why Resize Instead of Cropping?

Cropping cuts away parts of the image to change its shape, which means you lose content along the edges. Resizing keeps the entire image intact — every part of the scene stays in the frame, just at a different scale. When you want to preserve the full picture while changing its dimensions, resizing is the right choice.

### Proportional Scaling

This tool lets you enter a width, a height, or both. When you set only one dimension, the other adjusts automatically to maintain the image's original proportions. This prevents the image from looking stretched or squished. If you set both dimensions, the image will scale to fit those exact measurements.

This resizer runs entirely in your browser. When you select an image, your device processes the resize locally — your file is never uploaded to any server. Enter your target dimensions, and the resized image is ready to download in seconds. The tool accepts JPG, PNG, and WebP formats.
