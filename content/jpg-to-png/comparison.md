## JPG vs PNG — When to Use Each

Both JPG and PNG are popular image formats, but they serve different purposes. Here is a quick breakdown of how they compare.

<tr><td class="feature-cell"><span class="feature-cell__inner"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-maximize"></use></svg>File size</span></td><td data-label="JPG">Smaller</td><td data-label="PNG">Larger</td></tr>

    <div class="panel__head">
      <span class="panel__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-photo"></use></svg></span>
      <h3>Keep JPG When</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You are sharing personal photos by email or messaging apps</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need to keep file sizes as small as possible</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>The image is a photograph with no need for transparency</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You are uploading to social media, which often converts to JPG anyway</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Storage space is a concern and slight quality loss is acceptable</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="panel panel--accent">
    <div class="panel__head">
      <span class="panel__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-arrow-convert"></use></svg></span>
      <h3>Convert to PNG When</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need a transparent background on your image</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You are working with logos, icons, or graphics with sharp edges</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You plan to edit the image multiple times and want to preserve quality</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>The image contains text or fine lines that need to stay crisp</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You are preparing assets for a website, app, or design project</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You want to use the image as an overlay or layer in another design</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
