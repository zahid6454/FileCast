## What Is JPG?

JPG (also written as JPEG) is the most common image format in the world. Nearly every photo you take on your phone, download from the internet, or receive in an email is a JPG. It works by compressing images to keep file sizes small, which makes it perfect for storing and sharing photos quickly.

The trade-off is that JPG compression removes some image data each time you save. For everyday photos, this is barely noticeable. But for images that need crisp edges, text, or transparent backgrounds, it can be a problem.

### What Is PNG?

PNG is an image format built for quality and flexibility. Unlike JPG, PNG uses lossless compression — meaning it keeps every detail of your image intact, no matter how many times you save it. PNG also supports transparency, so you can have images with see-through backgrounds instead of a solid white or colored fill.

This makes PNG the go-to choice for logos, icons, graphics, screenshots, and any image where sharpness and transparency matter.
