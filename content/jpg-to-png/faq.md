## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to convert my images here?

Yes, completely. This tool runs the entire conversion inside your browser, which means your images never leave your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server, nothing is stored, and no one else can see your files. Once you close the page, the conversion data is gone. Your privacy is fully protected.

### Will the PNG file be larger than the original JPG?

In most cases, yes. PNG files are typically larger than JPG files because PNG uses lossless compression that keeps every detail intact, while JPG sacrifices some data to achieve smaller sizes. The exact difference depends on the image, but you might see the file size increase by two to five times. This is normal and is the trade-off for better quality and transparency support.

### Does converting to PNG improve the image quality?

Converting to PNG preserves the quality your JPG currently has, but it cannot recover details that were already lost during JPG compression. Think of it like making a perfect copy of a document — the copy is just as good as the original, but it cannot undo any smudges that were already there. The benefit is that once your image is in PNG format, no further quality will be lost when you save or edit it.

### Can I add a transparent background after converting?

Converting from JPG to PNG gives you a file that supports transparency, but it does not automatically remove the existing background. To make the background transparent, you would need to use a background removal tool after the conversion. The PNG format simply makes transparency possible — it is like unlocking the door, but you still need to walk through it.

### Can I convert multiple files at once?

Currently, this tool converts one file at a time. Drop a file, convert it, download, then click "Convert Another" for the next one. Batch conversion is planned for a future update.
