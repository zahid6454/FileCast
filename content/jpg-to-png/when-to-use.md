### Adding Transparency for Logos and Graphics

JPG files always have a solid background — usually white. If you have a logo or graphic that you need to place on top of other content, converting to PNG is the first step. PNG supports transparent backgrounds, so your image can sit cleanly over any color, pattern, or photo without an awkward white box around it.

### Preserving Quality for Editing

Every time you open, edit, and save a JPG, it loses a tiny bit of quality. Over several rounds of editing, this adds up and your image can start to look blurry or blocky. Converting to PNG before you start editing locks in the current quality. From that point on, you can save as many times as you need without any further loss.

### Using Images in Design Tools

Design applications like Canva, Figma, and Photoshop work best with PNG files, especially when you are layering images or need precise control over transparency. Converting your JPG to PNG before importing it into a design tool gives you more flexibility and cleaner results.

</div>

### Layering Images in Presentations and Projects

When you are building a slide deck, poster, or any visual project, you often need to stack images on top of each other. PNG files with transparent backgrounds let you layer elements without covering up what is underneath. This is essential for creating professional-looking compositions.

### Creating Web Graphics with Transparent Backgrounds

Website headers, buttons, icons, and product images often need transparent backgrounds so they blend naturally with the page design. If you have a JPG version of a graphic and need it for your website, converting to PNG ensures it will look right no matter what background color or image is behind it.
