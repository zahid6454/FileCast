## What Is PDF Crop?

PDF Crop trims the margin off every page of a PDF, removing excess white space around the actual content. The underlying page content doesn't move — the visible boundary of the page is just pulled in closer to what's on it.

This is different from cropping an image, where pixels outside the crop area are permanently discarded. A cropped PDF page still contains everything it did before; only the visible/printable area changes.

### Why Crop a PDF?

PDFs exported from slides, scanned documents, or web pages often have far more margin than the content needs — a slide deck exported at 16:9 with content centered in a smaller area, or a scan with wide borders around a single page of text. That excess margin wastes space when printed, and makes the content look small and hard to read on screen.

Trimming it tightens the page around the actual content, which is especially useful before printing on smaller paper or embedding a page as an image elsewhere.
