## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to compress my PDF here?

Yes. Your file is encrypted during upload, processed on our server, and deleted immediately after the compression completes. We never store, log, or read the content of your documents. No account is required and no conversion history is kept.

### Will compression affect text quality?

No. Text in a PDF remains sharp regardless of the compression level you choose. Compression primarily affects embedded images by reducing their resolution. Choose a higher quality setting if your document contains important photographs or detailed graphics.

### How much smaller will my file be?

It depends on the content. Image-heavy PDFs (scanned documents, photo albums, presentations with graphics) can shrink by 60-90% at maximum compression. Text-heavy PDFs with few images may only shrink by 5-15% since the text is already compact.

### Is there a file size limit?

Yes, the maximum file size is 25MB. If your PDF exceeds this limit, it may already be a candidate for compression — try splitting it into smaller parts using the PDF Split tool, compressing each part separately, then merging the results.

### Can I compress multiple PDFs at once?

Currently, this tool compresses one file at a time. Upload your PDF, download the compressed result, then start another compression for the next file.
