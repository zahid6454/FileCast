## Common Scenarios for PDF Compression

### Emailing Large Attachments

Most email services limit attachments to 10-25MB. If your PDF exceeds that limit, compression can bring it below the threshold without needing to use a file-sharing service. Choose maximum or balanced compression for the best size reduction.

### Uploading to Web Portals

Job application portals, government submission systems, and university upload forms often have strict file size limits. Compressing your PDF ensures it meets the requirement while preserving all the content.

### Reducing Storage Usage

Archives of scanned documents, photo collections exported as PDFs, and accumulated reports can consume significant storage space. Batch-compressing these files reclaims disk space without losing the content you need.

### Speeding Up Document Loading

Large PDFs load slowly, especially on mobile devices or slow connections. Compressed files open faster, scroll more smoothly, and provide a better reading experience for anyone viewing the document.

### Sharing Scanned Documents

Scanned PDFs are often 5-20x larger than they need to be because scanners default to high-resolution settings. Compressing a scanned document typically achieves 60-80% size reduction with no visible loss in text readability.
