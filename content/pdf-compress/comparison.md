<div class="panel-grid">
  <div class="panel panel--accent">
    <div class="panel__head">
      <span class="panel__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-minimize"></use></svg></span>
      <h3>Use Maximum Compression When</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You need the smallest possible file and image quality isn't critical.</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Scanned documents being emailed, forms for online submission, and reference copies that just need to be readable.</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Text remains sharp at any compression level — only images are affected.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="panel panel--neutral">
    <div class="panel__head">
      <span class="panel__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-sliders"></use></svg></span>
      <h3>Use Balanced or Moderate Compression When</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>You want a good balance between file size and visual quality.</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Balanced works well for documents shared digitally — reports, presentations, and proposals.</li>
      <li><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg>Moderate is the right choice when the document might be printed on a standard office printer.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
