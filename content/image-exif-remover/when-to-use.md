## Common Scenarios for Removing Photo Metadata

<div class="scenario-list" markdown="1">
<div class="scenario" markdown="1">
<div class="scenario__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-user"></use></svg></div>
<div class="scenario__body" markdown="1">

### Selling Items Online

Marketplace listings often include photos taken at home. Without removing GPS metadata first, anyone who downloads your listing photos can potentially see exactly where they were taken — worth stripping before you post, especially for high-value items.

</div>
</div>
<div class="scenario" markdown="1">
<div class="scenario__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-upload-cloud"></use></svg></div>
<div class="scenario__body" markdown="1">

### Posting on Social Media or Forums

Even if a platform strips metadata on its own end, not all of them do, and you can't always tell which ones do. Removing metadata yourself before uploading means you're not relying on the platform to protect that information for you.

</div>
</div>
<div class="scenario" markdown="1">
<div class="scenario__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-check"></use></svg></div>
<div class="scenario__body" markdown="1">

### Sharing Photos With Someone You Don't Know Well

Sending a photo to a new online contact, a stranger on a marketplace, or anyone outside your immediate circle is a reasonable moment to strip identifying metadata first, just as a privacy habit.

</div>
</div>
<div class="scenario" markdown="1">
<div class="scenario__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-code"></use></svg></div>
<div class="scenario__body" markdown="1">

### Publishing Images on a Website

Website images don't need embedded camera and GPS data — it only adds unnecessary file size with no benefit to visitors. If you're also trying to shrink your images for faster page loads, pair this with FileCast's [Image Compressor](/convert/image-compress/).

</div>
</div>
<div class="scenario" markdown="1">
<div class="scenario__icon"><svg aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><use href="#icon-file-plus"></use></svg></div>
<div class="scenario__body" markdown="1">

### Sharing Documents That Include Photos

If you're assembling scanned photos or images into a shareable document — for example with FileCast's [Image to PDF](/convert/image-to-pdf/) — it's worth cleaning metadata from the source photos first, since a PDF built from them can still carry that data through.
